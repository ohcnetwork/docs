# Change items in an open dispense order

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />

This flow describes how to change the [medication dispenses](../../../concepts/medications/medication-dispense.mdx) of an open [dispense order](../../../concepts/medications/dispense-order.mdx) at a pharmacy location before you complete it. You can change the lot, the quantity or the product of a dispense, change its status, or add a medicine to the order.

## Pre-requisites

- The dispense order is Draft or In Progress.
- If you change the lot, quantity or product of a dispense, the dispense has no invoice, or only a draft invoice.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Write Medication Dispense | Change a dispense, change its status, and add a medicine to the order. You must hold it in a department of the pharmacy location. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Pharmacist in Care | Open the dispense order. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Medication Dispense Read | See the dispenses of the order. You must hold it in a department of the pharmacy location. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Inventory Item | See the lots and their stock at the pharmacy location. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Product Knowledge | Search for a product when you substitute or add a medicine. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Invoice | See the invoices of the order, and let Care find the draft invoice for the new charges. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Write Invoice | Let Care add the new charges to the draft invoice. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Account | See the patient's account from the order. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Open the pages of the pharmacy location, including the dispense order. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Doctor, Facility Admin, Administrator, Nurse, Staff and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |

Without "Pharmacist in Care", a user needs "Can Read Supply Delivery" in a department of the pharmacy location, to open the order.

## Steps

### 1. Open the dispense order

Open the dispense order on **Dispense Medicines**.

To find the order, select **Pharmacy** > ℞ **Dispense** in the location sidebar. On the **Pending** tab, select **View Order** on the order. See [Find and print a dispense order](./find-dispense-order.mdx).

![The Dispense Orders page with the View Order button of a Draft order outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-1.png)

### 2. Change the lot, quantity or product of a dispense

1. Select **Edit Dispense** (the pencil) on the row.
2. Change the lot in **Select Lot**, or change the **Quantity**.
3. If the dispense is for a prescribed medicine, you can dispense another product. Select **Substitute**, and fill in the **Substitute Medication** sheet.
4. Select **Save Changes**. Care shows **Dispense updated successfully**.

Note: **Edit Dispense** shows only for a dispense in Preparation, In Progress, On Hold or Completed. The dispense must have no invoice, or only a draft invoice.

Note: If you keep the same lot, the new quantity can be up to the lot's stock plus the dispensed quantity.

![The edit button of the Omeprazole dispense outlined in the Draft order](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-2.png)

![The Edit Dispense sheet with the Quantity field outlined, changed to 7](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-3.png)

### 3. Change the status of a dispense

Select the new status in the **Status** column of the row. Care shows **Medication dispense status updated**.

You can select Preparation, In Progress, On Hold or Completed.

If the dispense has no invoice or only a draft invoice, you can also select Cancelled, Entered in Error, Stopped or Declined.

Note: You cannot change a dispense that is cancelled, entered in error, stopped or declined.

If you cancel a dispense that has a charge, Care takes the charge off the draft invoice. Care marks the medicine as not dispensed, and the quantity returns to stock.

![The status list of the Amlodipine dispense open, with In Progress outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-4.png)

### 4. Add a medicine to the order

1. Below the tables, search for the product in **Add Medication**. Care opens the **Add Medication** sheet.
2. Check the **Dosage** and the **Frequency**. These fields are mandatory. Care fills in a dose of 1, in the unit of the product, and the frequency "SOS (As needed)".
3. If the medicine is not taken as needed, select the frequency and enter the **Duration**. Care disables **Duration** while the frequency is "SOS (As needed)".
4. Check the **Required Quantity**, the lots and their quantities. Care calculates the quantity from the dose, the frequency and the duration.
5. Select **Add Medication**. Care shows **Medication added successfully**.

Note: **Add Medication** shows only when the new charges can go on an invoice that can still change.

This is the case when the order has a draft invoice or no invoice yet. It is also the case when the order has dispenses that are not on an invoice.

![The Add Medication sheet with Paracetamol selected and a lot already selected](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-5.png)

![The new Paracetamol dispense outlined at the top of the order, with the message Medication added successfully](/media/medications/medication-dispense/change-dispense-order-items-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- When you edit a dispense, Care cancels the original dispense. Care creates a new dispense in the same order, with the same status.
- The new dispense has the lot, quantity or product that you chose.
- Care marks the new dispense as fully dispensed, so a prescribed medicine counts as dispensed.
- When you add a medicine, Care creates one dispense for each lot, with the status Preparation and no prescription.
- If the order has a draft invoice, Care adds the new charges to it.

Next, collect the payment for the new charges and complete the order. See [Collect payment and complete a dispense order](./complete-dispense-order.mdx).
