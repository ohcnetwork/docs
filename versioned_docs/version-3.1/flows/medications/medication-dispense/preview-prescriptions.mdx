# Preview a patient's prescriptions

## Overview

This flow describes how to check a patient's [prescriptions](../../../concepts/medications/medication-request.mdx) in the **Prescription Preview** at a pharmacy location before you bill them. It also describes how to complete a prescription without billing, and how to reactivate a completed or cancelled prescription.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has a prescription at your facility.
- You can open the **Prescription Queue** at the pharmacy location. See [Open the prescription queue](./open-prescription-queue.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Pharmacist in Care | Open the queue and the prescriptions, mark a prescription as completed, and reactivate a completed or cancelled prescription. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Healthcare Service | Open the facility's list of services to reach the pharmacy location. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Open the pharmacy location and its pages, including the **Prescription Preview**. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Doctor, Facility Admin, Administrator, Nurse, Staff and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Cancel a prescription while its encounter is open. You must hold it in a department of the encounter or of its current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. The Pharmacist role does not. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the preview

Open the preview from the **Prescription Queue** in one of these ways:

- Select **View Rx** on a row to open one prescription.
- Select a patient's name, select the prescriptions, and then select **Preview Selected Rx**.

Care opens the **Prescription Preview** with the patient's details at the top.

If the patient has other unbilled prescriptions in this facility, Care shows how many. To open all of them, select **Show all**.

![The View Rx link of a queue row outlined in the Prescription Queue](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-1.png)

![The Prescription Preview page with the patient header outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-2.png)

### 2. Check the medicines

To find a medicine, type its name in **Search Medications**.

To filter the medicines, select a **Dispense Status**: **All**, **Incomplete**, **Partial** or **Complete**.

Above the table, Care counts the prescriptions by the dispense status of their active medicines.

Each prescription shows **Prescribed by**, the date and time, the number of items, the patient's current **Location** and any **Note**. A completed or cancelled prescription also shows its status.

The table shows these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Medicine** | The prescribed medicine. |
| Dose · Schedule · Duration / Instructions | The dosage instructions of the medicine. |
| **Total to Dispense** | The total quantity of the medicine to dispense. |
| **Dispense Status** | **Incomplete**, **Partial** or **Complete**. A medicine with no dispense status shows **Incomplete**. |
| **Status** | The status of the medicine. |

![The Search Medications box outlined, with Omeprazole typed and only that medicine shown](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-3.png)

![A prescription card outlined, showing the prescriber, the note, and each medicine with its dose, total to dispense, dispense status and status](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-4.png)

### 3. Print the prescriptions

Select **Print Prescription**. When more than one prescription is open, this button is **Print All Prescriptions**.

To print one prescription, select the print icon of that prescription.

![The Print Prescription button outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-5.png)

### 4. Complete or reactivate a prescription

Open the menu of the prescription.

- If the prescription is active and you do not bill it, select **Mark as Completed**. Care shows **Prescription completed successfully**.
- If the prescription is completed or cancelled, select **Reactivate Prescription** to make it active again. Care shows **Prescription reactivated successfully**.

Note: With "Pharmacist in Care", you can complete or reactivate a prescription after the encounter is closed.

Note: The menu of an active prescription also shows **Cancel Prescription**. Care accepts it only from a user who can update the encounter's clinical data, while the encounter is open. By default, the Pharmacist role cannot cancel a prescription.

![The menu of a prescription open, with Mark as Completed outlined above Cancel Prescription](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-6.png)

![A completed prescription with its menu open and Reactivate Prescription outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-7.png)

![The message Prescription reactivated successfully on the Prescription Preview page](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-8.png)

### 5. Start billing

Select **Start Billing**. Care opens **Bill Medications** with the active prescriptions that are open in the preview. See [Bill and dispense prescribed medicines](./dispense-prescribed-medicines.mdx).

To bill one prescription, open its menu and select **Bill Prescription**.

Note: **Start Billing** shows only when at least one open prescription is active. Press `b` to start billing.

![The Bill Medications page, with the medicines of the prescription outlined, each with its lot, quantity and price](/media/medications/medication-dispense/preview-prescriptions-9.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows the selected prescriptions, with the dispense status of each medicine.
- If you completed a prescription, it moves to the **Completed** tab of the queue.
- If you reactivated a prescription, it returns to the **Billing Pending** tab of the queue.
