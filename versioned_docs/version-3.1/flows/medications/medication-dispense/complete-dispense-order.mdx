# Collect payment and complete a dispense order

## Overview

<video src="/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order.mp4" poster="/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to collect the payment for a [dispense order](../../../concepts/medications/dispense-order.mdx) at a pharmacy location, and then complete the order. When you complete the order, Care sets the order and its dispenses to Completed.

## Pre-requisites

- You billed the medicines, and the dispense order is Draft or In Progress. See [Bill and dispense prescribed medicines](./dispense-prescribed-medicines.mdx) or [Dispense medicines without a prescription](./dispense-without-prescription.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Pharmacist in Care | Open the dispense order and change its status, including **Complete Dispense**. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Write Medication Dispense | Let Care set the dispenses to Completed with the order. You must hold it in a department of the pharmacy location. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Medication Dispense Read | See the dispenses of the order. You must hold it in a department of the pharmacy location. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Write Payment Reconciliation | Record the payment. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Write Invoice | Issue the invoice, and mark it as balanced. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Invoice | See the invoice and its payment status on the order. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Account | See the patient's account from the order. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Open the pages of the pharmacy location, including the dispense order. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Doctor, Facility Admin, Administrator, Nurse, Staff and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |

By default, the Pharmacist role, held in a department of the pharmacy location, holds every permission in this table.

Without "Pharmacist in Care", a user needs "Can Read Supply Delivery" to open the order and "Can Create Supply Delivery on Facility" to change its status, both in a department of the pharmacy location. By default, only the Facility Admin role holds "Can Create Supply Delivery on Facility".

## Steps

### 1. Open the dispense order

After you bill the medicines, Care opens the dispense order.

To open the order later, select **Pharmacy** > ℞ **Dispense** in the location sidebar. On the **Pending** tab, select **View Order** on the order. See [Find and print a dispense order](./find-dispense-order.mdx).

Care opens **Dispense Medicines** with the status of the order at the top.

If the order is Draft, select **Mark as In Progress** next to the status.

![The Dispense Orders page with the View Order button of a Draft order outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-1.png)

![The Draft order open, with the Mark as In Progress button next to its status outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-2.png)

### 2. Check the dispenses

Care groups the dispenses by invoice. Dispenses that are not yet on an invoice come first. Cancelled dispenses are last, under **Cancelled Dispenses**.

The table shows these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Medicine** | The medicine, with its lot and expiry. |
| **Instructions** | The dosage instructions. |
| **Quantity** | The quantity dispensed. |
| **Status** | The status of the dispense. |
| **Actions** | The actions for the dispense, while the order is open. |

To change a dispense before you complete the order, see [Change items in an open dispense order](./change-dispense-order-items.mdx).

![The dispenses of the order outlined, each In Progress, with the medicine, batch, instructions and quantity](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-3.png)

### 3. Issue the invoice

Do this step only if the invoice is not issued yet.

- If the banner shows **Invoice Not Generated**, select **Issue Invoice**. Care creates the invoice, issues it, and opens the payment sheet.
- If the banner shows **Draft Invoice**, select **Issue Invoice**.

Note: **Invoice Not Generated** shows when the order has charges that are not on an invoice yet. This happens, for example, when Care could not create the invoice when you billed. **Issue Invoice** shows only when the patient has an account.

![The Issue Invoice button outlined in the Invoice Not Generated banner](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-4.png)

### 4. Collect the payment

The payment banner shows **Payment Pending**, the **Invoice Total** and the **Amount Due**.

Select **Collect Payment**, and record the payment in the payment sheet. See [Record a payment](../../billing/payment-reconciliation/record-payment.mdx).

Note: Right after you bill, Care opens the payment sheet for you. Press `Enter` to select **Collect Payment**.

When the payments on the invoice equal its total, Care marks the invoice as balanced. Care shows **Invoice marked as balanced**.

To settle the invoice without full payment, open the menu of the payment banner and select **Mark as balanced**. See [Mark an invoice as balanced](../../billing/invoice/mark-invoice-balanced.mdx).

If the banner shows **Cash Collected** but the invoice is not balanced, select **Mark as balanced**.

Note: If the invoice also holds charges that are not medicines, the banner says so. Select **View Invoice** to see all the charges.

![The Collect Payment sheet with the amount paid, the amount received and the change to return](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-5.png)

![The Collect Payment sheet showing Payment Recorded Successfully, with the amount received and the payment method](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-6.png)

### 5. Complete the dispense

When every charge of the order is settled in a balanced invoice, the footer says **Payment has been collected.**

Select **Complete Dispense**.

Note: Press `Shift+Enter` to complete the dispense.

Note: **Complete Dispense** is disabled while the footer says **Settle all pending invoices before completing the dispense.** Collect the payment, or mark the invoice as balanced.

![The Complete Dispense button outlined, with the payment status Fully Paid](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-7.png)

![The order at Status: Completed, with the question Next pharmacy action? outlined beside Medication Return, Print and Prescription Queue](/media/medications/medication-dispense/complete-dispense-order-8.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care sets the dispense order to Completed, and sets its open dispenses to Completed. Cancelled dispenses stay cancelled.
- Care shows **Dispense Completed!** and **Next pharmacy action?**.
- From there, you can select **Print** to print the order, or **Prescription Queue** to go back to the queue.
- To take back medicines later, select **Medication Return**. A return needs "Can Write Medication Returns", which by default only the Facility Admin role holds. See [Return dispensed medicines](./return-medicines.mdx).
