# Dispense from an encounter

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />

This flow describes how to [dispense](../../../concepts/medications/medication-dispense.mdx) medicines and other products of the facility to a patient from an encounter, from the stock of a location.

To bill and dispense prescribed medicines at a pharmacy location, see [Bill and dispense prescribed medicines](./dispense-prescribed-medicines.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has an encounter at the facility. The encounter is not completed, cancelled, discontinued or entered in error.
- You open the encounter from the facility. If you select a past encounter of the patient, Care does not show **Dispense**.
- The items that you want to dispense are in stock at the location that you dispense from.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Write Medication Dispense | Save the dispensed items. You must hold it in a department of the location that you dispense from. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Pharmacist in Care | Let Care complete the dispense order after it saves the items. You need this permission or "Can Create Supply Delivery on Facility". You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Create Supply Delivery on Facility | Let Care complete the dispense order, if you do not have "Pharmacist in Care". You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, only the Facility Admin role holds this permission. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Open the encounter, the **Medications** tab and **Dispense**. You need this permission or "Can Read encounter related clinical data". It counts when you hold it for the patient, for example in a department of an open encounter of the patient. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Open the encounter, the **Medications** tab and **Dispense**. You must hold it in a department of the encounter or of its current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can List Facility Locations | See a location in the location selector. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Doctor, Facility Admin, Administrator, Nurse, Staff and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Product Knowledge | Search for the products of the facility in **Add Item**. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Inventory Item | See the lots of a product at the location. You must hold it in a department of the location. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Write Invoice | Let Care create an invoice after the dispense. You need it only if your deployment creates an invoice after a dispense. You must hold it in a department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |

Note: By default, the Staff, Doctor and Nurse roles can save the dispensed items. They cannot complete the dispense order, so the order stays Draft.

Note: By default, the Pharmacist role cannot open an encounter, so it cannot use this flow. See [Bill and dispense prescribed medicines](./dispense-prescribed-medicines.mdx).

## Steps

### 1. Open the Dispense History sub-tab

1. Open the encounter.
2. Select the **Medications** tab.
3. Select the **Dispense History** sub-tab.

Note: Press `g` then `m` to go to the **Medications** tab.

![The Dispense History sub-tab outlined on the Medications tab](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-1.png)

### 2. Select Dispense

Select **Dispense**. Care opens the location selector.

Note: You can also press `Shift+D` in the encounter.

![The Dispense button outlined under No dispense history found](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-2.png)

### 3. Select the location to dispense from

At first, the location selector lists only your own locations.

To find another location, search for it by name. You can also select a location to open the locations under it.

Select the location that holds the stock. Care opens the **Dispense** drawer.

The drawer shows the location under **Selected Location**. To change the location, select **Selected Location**.

Note: If you already added items, Care asks **Change Location?**. Select **Discard & Switch** to change the location and remove the items. Select **Stay here** to keep the items.

![The location selector with Pharmacy typed and the Pharmacy location outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-3.png)

![The Dispense sheet with Pharmacy under Selected Location, outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-4.png)

### 4. Add the items

1. Select **Add Item**.
2. Search for the product and select it. Care adds a row for the item.
3. Care selects the lot that expires first, and skips lots that are expired or expire soon. Care sets the quantity to 1.
4. To change the lot, select **Select Lot**. You can select more than one lot for the same item.
5. Enter the **Quantity** for each lot that you selected.

Select **Add Item** again for each other item that you want to dispense.

Care shows these columns for each item:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Items** | The name of the product. |
| **Select Lot** | The lots of that product that you selected at the location. |
| **Quantity** | The quantity that you dispense from each lot. |
| **Base Amount** | The base price of each lot. |
| **Expiry** | The expiry month and year of each lot. |
| **Actions** | The button that removes the item from the list. |

Note: If the location holds no stock of an item, Care shows **No Stock Available**. Care does not dispense until you remove that item.

Note: Care does not let you dispense more than the stock of a lot.

Note: Which lots count as expiring soon depends on your deployment's configuration.

![A Paracetamol row in the Dispense sheet outlined, with its lot, quantity, base amount and expiry](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-5.png)

### 5. Confirm the dispense

Select **Confirm Dispense**. Care shows **Items dispensed successfully**.

Note: Press `Shift+Enter` to confirm the dispense.

![The Confirm Dispense button outlined](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-6.png)

![The message Items dispensed successfully outlined on the Dispense History sub-tab](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-7.png)

![The new dispense order selected, with the Paracetamol dispense outlined in the table at the status Completed](/media/medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication-8.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care records a dispense for each lot, with the status Completed, and reduces the stock at the location.
- Care groups the dispenses into one dispense order. If you have "Pharmacist in Care" or "Can Create Supply Delivery on Facility", Care sets the order to Completed. Otherwise the order stays Draft.
- If a product has a charge item definition, Care creates a charge item for it on the patient's account. By default, Care does not create an invoice.
- A user with "Pharmacist in Care" sees the dispenses in the **Dispense History** sub-tab. See [View dispense history for an encounter](./view-dispense-history.mdx).

Note: Your deployment can create an invoice after each dispense. Then Care creates and issues an invoice for the charge items on the patient's active account, and opens it. This setting is off by default.
