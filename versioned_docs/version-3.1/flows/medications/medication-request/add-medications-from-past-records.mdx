# Add medicines from past records

## Overview

This flow describes how to copy medicines from a patient's past prescriptions and [medication statements](../../../concepts/medications/medication-statement.mdx) into a new prescription. You do this in the same form that you use to prescribe a [medication request](../../../concepts/medications/medication-request.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- You can prescribe medicines in the encounter. See the pre-requisites in [Prescribe medicines](./prescribe-medication.mdx).
- You open the form to create a prescription. **Medication History** is not on the form when you edit a prescription.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Load **Past Prescriptions** and **Medication Statements** in **Medication History**. You must hold it for the patient, because the lists include all of the patient's encounters. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Save the medicines. You must hold it in one of the encounter's departments, or in a department of the encounter's current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |

Note: Read access to this encounter alone does not load **Medication History**. Care gives you access to the patient through the patient's open encounters, the patient's organizations, or a direct link to the patient. A closed encounter does not count.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Medication Request form

Open the form to create a prescription, as in [Prescribe medicines](./prescribe-medication.mdx).

![The encounter Overview tab with the Medication quick action outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-1.png)

### 2. Open the medication history

Select **Medication History**.

![The Medication History sheet with the Past Prescriptions tab outlined, listing earlier medicines with their dosage, duration and prescriber](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-2.png)

### 3. Select the past records

1. Select the tab that has the records.
2. Select each record that you want to add.
3. Select **Add Selected**.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| Past Prescriptions | The medicines prescribed for the patient in all encounters, in every status except Entered in Error. |
| Medication Statements | The medication statements of the patient, in every status except Entered in Error. |

Each tab groups the records under the date they were created, newest first.

Note: **Add Selected** adds only the records that you selected on the open tab.

![Two past prescriptions selected in the Medication History sheet, one row outlined, and 2 Past Prescriptions Selected at the bottom](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-3.png)

![The Add Selected button outlined, with two past prescriptions selected](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-4.png)

### 4. Complete the new rows

Care adds each record to the form as a new medicine.

- A medicine from **Past Prescriptions** comes with its dosage and other details. Care records you as its requester.
- A medication statement brings only the medicine and its note. Enter the **Dosage** and the **Frequency**.

To change other fields, follow steps 3 to 6 of [Prescribe medicines](./prescribe-medication.mdx).

![The Medication Request form with new rows for Metformin and Omeprazole, the Duration field of the first row outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-5.png)

### 5. Save the medicines

Select **Save Changes**.

![The Medication Request form with the Save Changes button outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-6.png)

![The encounter Overview tab after the save, with the message Questionnaire submitted successfully outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/add-medications-from-past-records-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows "Questionnaire submitted successfully" and opens the **Overview** tab of the encounter.
- The added medicines are part of the new prescription of this save, with any other medicines that you added.
- Care copies the records. The earlier medicines and medication statements do not change.
