# Prescribe medicines

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />

This flow describes how to prescribe one or more medicines for a patient in an encounter. Each time you save, Care groups the medicines that you saved into one new prescription. See [medication request](../../../concepts/medications/medication-request.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has an encounter in your facility, and you open that encounter from the facility. Care shows an encounter that you open from an organization as read-only.
- The status of the encounter is not Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued or Entered in error.
- If you view an earlier encounter inside the encounter page, go back to the encounter that you opened. Care lets you prescribe only in that encounter.
- If another user is the requester, that user also has "Update Encounter related clinical data" for this encounter.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Save the medicines. You must hold it in one of the encounter's departments, or in a department of the encounter's current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Open the encounter and see the **Medications** tab, the **Create** button and the **Medication** quick action. You need this permission for the patient, or the next permission. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Gives the same access as the permission above, when you hold it in one of the encounter's departments, or in a department of the encounter's current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Product Knowledge | Search the **Product** tab of the medicine picker. You can hold it in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the Medication Request form

Open the form in one of these ways:

- Open the **Medications** tab of the encounter. On the **Prescriptions** sub-tab, select **All Prescriptions**. Then select **Create**.
- On the **Overview** tab of the encounter, select the **Medication** quick action.
- Select **Encounter Actions**. Then select **Add Medication**.

Care opens the "Medication Request" form for the encounter.

Note: Press `k` to open the form while the **Overview** tab shows the **Medication** quick action.

Note: When a prescription is selected on the **Prescriptions** sub-tab, the button reads **Edit** and changes that prescription. See [Edit a prescription](./update-prescription.mdx).

![The Medications tab of an encounter, with All Prescriptions selected and the Create button outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-1.png)

### 2. Select the medicine

1. Select the **Add Medication** field. After the first medicine, the field reads **Add another Medication**.
2. Select the tab to search in.
3. Select the medicine.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| Product | The active products in the facility's product catalogue. Until you type, Care lists the product categories. Select a category to see its products. |
| Medication | The clinical drug list. |

On a large screen, Care adds the medicine to the form as a row. On a small screen, Care opens the medicine in a sheet. Complete the sheet and confirm it.

If you select a consumable product, Care sets its first dosage step to "as needed".

Note: The picker opens on the **Product** tab, unless your deployment's configuration sets another default.

Note: To copy medicines from the patient's past records, see [Add medicines from past records](./add-medications-from-past-records.mdx).

Note: The form also has a **Templates** button for saved prescription templates.

![The medicine picker on the Product tab, with Amox typed in the search box and Amoxicillin outlined in the results](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-2.png)

### 3. Enter the dosage, frequency and duration

Complete the fields in the row of each medicine.

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Dosage | The amount of the medicine for each dose. Required. |
| Frequency | How often to give the medicine, such as "1-0-1". Required. |
| Duration | How long to give the medicine. Optional. |

In **Duration**, enter a duration, such as "5 days". You can also select **Set a range of days** or **Set fixed start & end dates**.

The **Frequency** list also offers "SOS (As needed)" and "STAT (Immediately)".

If you select "SOS (As needed)", Care disables **Duration**.

If a required field is empty, Care shows "This field is required".

If a duration is not valid, Care shows "Enter a valid duration".

![A medicine row for Amoxicillin with the dosage, the frequency 1-1-1 (Thrice a day) and the duration 5 days filled in, the Duration field outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-3.png)

### 4. Set a changing dose

This step is optional.

1. To give a dose that changes over time, select **Configure dose range** beside the dose.
2. In **Taper & Titrate Dosage**, enter the **Start Dose** and the **End Dose**.

To add another dosage step to a new medicine, select **Add Dosage Step** under its name.

To remove a dosage step, select **Remove step** on that step.

### 5. Complete the advanced fields

This step is optional.

- On a large screen, select the bar in the table header. Its tooltip reads **Show Advanced Fields**.
- On a small screen, expand **Advanced Fields** under the medicine.

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Instructions | Directions for the medicine. If the frequency is "SOS (As needed)", also select the reason in **Select reason for PRN**. |
| Route | The route to give the medicine. |
| Site | The body site for the medicine. |
| Method | The method to give the medicine. |
| Intent | The intent of the medication request. |
| Authored On | The date and time of the request. Care fills in the current date and time. You cannot select a future date. |
| Requester | The user who requests the medicine. Care fills in your name. You can select another user of the facility. |

If the form has more than one medicine, you can copy the requester to every medicine. Select **Apply this requester to all medications**.

Care then shows "Requester applied to all medications".

Note: The requester must have "Update Encounter related clinical data" for this encounter. Otherwise, Care saves none of the medicines.

### 6. Add notes

This step is optional.

- To add a note for one medicine, enter it in the note field of that medicine. The field shows "Enter additional notes".
- To add a note for the whole prescription, enter it in **Note**. The field shows "Add any notes for this prescription...".

Care shows the prescription **Note** field after you add the first medicine.

![The Note field of the medicine row outlined, with the text Take after food](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-4.png)

![The Note box of the prescription outlined, with the text Review after the course](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-5.png)

### 7. Save the medicines

Select **Save Changes**.

Note: Press `Shift+Enter` to save the form.

Note: You cannot save this form as a draft.

![The Medication Request form with the Save Changes button outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-6.png)

![The encounter Overview tab after the save, with the message Questionnaire submitted successfully outlined](/media/medications/medication-request/prescribe-medication-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows "Questionnaire submitted successfully".
- Care opens the **Overview** tab of the encounter.
- The medicines that you saved together form one new prescription.
- On **Medications** > **Prescriptions**, the new prescription shows the date and time of the save. It also shows "Prescribed by" with the name of the requester, and its tags.
- A prescription has no name. Each save makes a new prescription, and you cannot add medicines to a saved prescription.
- If Care cannot save one of the medicines, Care saves none of them.
