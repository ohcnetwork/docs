# Cancel a charge item

## Overview

This flow describes how to cancel a Billable [charge item](../../../concepts/billing/charge-item.mdx) in Care by marking it as Not Billable, Entered in Error or Aborted. You cannot undo a cancellation.

## Pre-requisites

- Someone added the charge item, and its status is Billable. Care shows the cancel options only for Billable charge items.
- If the charge item is on an invoice, its status is Billed or Paid. To make it Billable again, remove it from a draft invoice, or cancel the invoice. See [Change the items of a draft invoice](../invoice/edit-invoice-items.mdx) and [Cancel an invoice](../invoice/cancel-invoice.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Cancel Charge Item | Lets you cancel the charge item. Facility Admin holds it by default. |
| Can Update Charge Item | Care checks it for every status change, before it checks Can Cancel Charge Item. Facility Admin holds it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you open the account. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the **Invoices** tab, which the account page opens first. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Charge Item | Lets you see the charge items of the account. The same eight roles hold it by default. |

Care checks each permission through your role in any organization of the facility.

By default, every cancellation needs Can Cancel Charge Item. A deployment can set a period after a charge item is added. During that period, Can Update Charge Item is enough to cancel it.

If you do not have these permissions, Care refuses the change.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Charge Items tab of the account

In the facility sidebar, select **Billing**.

Select **Accounts**. Open the account of the patient. See [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx).

Select the **Charge Items** tab, or press `Shift+2`.

Note: For a charge item for a bed stay, you can also use the **Bed Charge Items** tab. The **Create Invoice** page of the account also has the same row menu.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the Charge Items tab of the account](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-1.png)

### 2. Open the row menu

On the row of the charge item, open the more-options (**⋯**) menu.

![Care screen for step 2, Open the row menu](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-2.png)

### 3. Choose the new status

Select one of these options:

- **Mark as 'Not Billable'**
- **Mark as 'Entered in Error'**
- **Mark as 'Aborted'**

Care opens the **Confirm Status Change** dialog. It warns that you cannot undo this action.

![Care screen for step 3, Choose the new status](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-3.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Choose the new status](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-4.png)

### 4. Confirm the change

Select the confirm button. It repeats the option that you chose, for example **Mark as 'Aborted'**.

![Care screen for step 4, Confirm the change](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-5.png)

![Care screen for step 4, Confirm the change](/media/billing/charge-item/cancel-charge-item-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows **Charge Item updated successfully**. The charge item shows the new status.
- Nobody can change the charge item after this.
- The charge item cannot go on an invoice.
- Cancelling a charge item does not update the **Total Billable** of the account at once. Care updates it when someone rebalances the account, or when an invoice or a payment of the account changes. See [Rebalance an account](../account/rebalance-account.mdx).
- When an appointment, a medication dispense or a service request is cancelled, Care sets its charge items to Aborted.
- If one of those charge items is on an invoice that is not a draft, Care refuses to cancel it: "Cannot cancel charge item in a non-draft invoice".
