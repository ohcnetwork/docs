# Move charge items to another account

## Overview

This flow describes how to move one or more Billable [charge items](../../../concepts/billing/charge-item.mdx) from one [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) to another account of the same patient at the facility.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has another account at the facility. That account can have any status and any billing status.
- The charge items that you want to move are Billable, and they are on no invoice.
- You move at most 100 charge items at a time.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create Charge Item | Lets you select charge items and move them. Without it, Care does not show the checkboxes or **Change Account**. Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you open the account and see the other accounts of the patient. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the **Invoices** tab, which the account page opens first. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Charge Item | Lets you see the charge items of the account. The same eight roles hold it by default. |

Care checks each permission through your role in any organization of the facility.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Charge Items tab of the account

In the facility sidebar, select **Billing**.

Select **Accounts**. Open the account that holds the charge items. See [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx).

Select the **Charge Items** tab, or press `Shift+2`.

Note: Use the **Charge Items** tab. The row menu on the **Bed Charge Items** tab has no **Change Account**.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the Charge Items tab of the account](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-1.png)

### 2. Select the charge items

Select the checkbox of each charge item that you want to move.

To select all the Billable charge items in the list, select the checkbox in the header row.

Note: Only Billable charge items have a checkbox that you can select. On other rows, the checkbox is disabled. Its tooltip says **Only billable charge items can be moved to another account**.

![Care screen for step 2, Select the charge items](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-2.png)

### 3. Start the move

In the bar that shows the number of **items selected**, select **Change Account**.

Note: To move one charge item, open the more-options (**⋯**) menu on its row. Select **Change Account**.

Note: To clear your selection, select **Clear Selection**.

![Care screen for step 3, Start the move](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-3.png)

### 4. Check the charge items

Care opens the **Change Account** form. Check the charge items and their totals.

![Care screen for step 4, Check the charge items](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-4.png)

### 5. Select the target account

Under **Select target account**, select the account that receives the charge items. Each account shows its name, service period and billing status.

Note: If the patient has no other account at the facility, Care shows **No other accounts available**.

![Care screen for step 5, Select the target account](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-5.png)

### 6. Move the charge items

Select **Change Account**. The button is not available until you select an account.

![Care screen for step 6, Move the charge items](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-6.png)

![Care screen for step 6, Move the charge items](/media/billing/charge-item/move-charge-items-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The charge items move to the target account. Care shows **Charge items moved to new account successfully**.
- Care calculates the totals again for the target account and for the account that the charge items came from.
- If Care cannot move the charge items, it shows the reason, or **Failed to change account for charge items**.
