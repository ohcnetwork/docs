# Rebalance an account

## Overview

This flow describes how to make Care calculate the totals of an [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) again: **Amount Due**, **Total Paid**, **Billed (Gross)**, and **Total Billable**.

Adding, editing, or cancelling a charge item does not update the totals. Rebalance the account to bring **Total Billable** up to date.

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the account page. See [View an account](./view-account.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update Account | Shows **Rebalance** and lets Care calculate the totals again. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin holds it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you open the account page. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the **Invoices** tab, which the account page opens first. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the account

Open the account page. See [View an account](./view-account.mdx).

### 2. Find the totals

Find the totals of the account on the account page.

If Care has calculated the totals before, it shows when it last did so, next to **Rebalance**.

![Care screen for step 2, Find the totals](/media/billing/account/rebalance-account-1.png)

### 3. Rebalance the account

Select **Rebalance**. While Care calculates the totals, the link shows **Rebalancing**.

Note: Care also calculates the totals again when a payment or an invoice changes. It does this when charge items move to another account, too.

![Care screen for step 3, Rebalance the account](/media/billing/account/rebalance-account-2.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Rebalance the account](/media/billing/account/rebalance-account-3.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows **Account rebalanced successfully**.
- Care calculates the totals again from the charge items, the invoices, and the payments of the account.
- Care records the time of the calculation.
- If the calculation fails, Care shows **Failed to rebalance account**.
