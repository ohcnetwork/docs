# View an account

## Overview

This flow describes how to find an [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) and open the account page. The account page shows the details, the totals, and the Billing Status of the account, and its invoices, charge items, payments, and reports.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- If you start from the patient, you can open the patient's profile in the facility. See [View a patient's profile](../../clinical/patient/view-patient-profile.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Account | Lets you see the account list and open an account. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the **Invoices** tab, which the account page opens first. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |
| Can Read Tag Config | Lets you use the **Tags** filter of the account list. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the account list

Use one of these entry points:

- In the **Administration** section of the facility sidebar, select **Billing**. Then select **Accounts**. Care shows the accounts of the facility.
- On the patient's profile, select the **Accounts** tab. Care shows the accounts of that patient at the facility.
- On the patient's home page, select **View Accounts**, or press `a`. Care shows the accounts of that patient.
- On an appointment, select **Accounts**, or press `4`. Care shows the accounts of the patient of the appointment.
- On an account page, select **Past Accounts**. Care shows the **Accounts** tab of the patient.

Note: You can also open an account from the **Account** card of an encounter. See [Link an encounter to an account](./link-encounter-to-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the account list](/media/billing/account/view-account-1.png)

### 2. Find the account

The list shows Active accounts when it opens.

To find other accounts, use the filters **Account Status**, **Period**, **Billing Status**, **Encounter Class**, and **Tags**.

Note: The **Period** filter uses the date when the account was created.

In **Billing** > **Accounts**, use **Filter by Identifier** to find the accounts of one patient.

The list shows these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | The name of the account. In **Billing** > **Accounts**, the name of the patient shows below it. |
| **Balance** | The Amount Due of the account, rounded. |
| **Billable** | The Total Billable of the account. |
| **Account Status** | The status of the account. |
| **Billing Status** | The Billing Status of the account. |
| **Period** | The start date of the account, and its end date if it has one. |
| **Tags** | The tags of the account. |
| **Action** | The **Go to account** button. |

If no account matches the filters, Care shows **No accounts found**.

![Care screen for step 2, Find the account](/media/billing/account/view-account-2.png)

### 3. Open the account

In the **Action** column, select **Go to account**. Care opens the account page on the **Invoices** tab.

Note: Selecting the row does not open the account.

If Care cannot load the account, it shows **Account not found** and a **Back to accounts** button.

![Care screen for step 3, Open the account](/media/billing/account/view-account-3.png)

### 4. Read the account details

The top of the account page shows the patient. Below it, Care shows these details:

| Component | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | The name of the account. |
| **Status** | The status of the account. |
| **Start date** | When the account starts. |
| **End date** | When the account ends. Care shows **Ongoing** if the account has no end date. |
| **Tags** | The tags of the account, and the **Add Tags** or **Manage Tags** button. |

![Care screen for step 4, Read the account details](/media/billing/account/view-account-4.png)

### 5. Read the totals

| Component | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount Due** | Billed (Gross) minus Total Paid. If the patient owes money, Care shows the amount in red with **Pending from Patient**. Otherwise Care shows it in green with **Overpaid amount to be refunded**. |
| **Total Paid** | The payments received on the account, minus the credit notes. |
| **Billed (Gross)** | The total of the invoices of the account, without cancelled invoices and invoices entered in error. |
| **Total Billable** | The total of the Billable charge items of the account. |

If Care has calculated the totals before, it shows when it last did so.

Adding, editing, or cancelling a charge item does not update the totals. To calculate the totals again, see [Rebalance an account](./rebalance-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 5, Read the totals](/media/billing/account/view-account-5.png)

### 6. Read the Billing Status

The **Billing Status** stepper shows the steps Open, Care Completed, Billing, and Closed.

The stepper shows the name of the current step and the next step. Point to another step to see its name.

If the account has a closed Billing Status, Closed is the current step.

![Care screen for step 6, Read the Billing Status](/media/billing/account/view-account-6.png)

### 7. Open a tab

Select a tab, or press its keyboard shortcut.

| Tab | What it shows | Keyboard shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invoices** | The invoices of the account. The page opens on this tab. See [Find and open an invoice](../invoice/view-invoice.mdx). | `Shift+1` |
| **Charge Items** | The charge items of the account. See [Add charge items](../charge-item/add-charge-items.mdx). | `Shift+2` |
| **Payments** | The payments of the account. See [Find payments](../payment-reconciliation/find-payments.mdx). | `Shift+3` |
| **Reports** | The reports of the account, and the **Generate Report** button. See [Generate and view a report](../../platform/report-template/generate-and-view-a-report.mdx). | `Shift+4` |
| **Bed Charge Items** | The bed charge items of the account. See [Add bed charges](../charge-item/add-bed-charges.mdx). | `Shift+5` |

Note: On narrow screens, some tabs move into a menu.

Note: Each linked flow lists the permissions for the content of its tab.

Note: The **Bed Charge Items** tab needs a primary encounter. If the account has none, the tab shows **No encounter associated**.

Note: To generate an account report, you need Can Update Account and Can generate report from template. The facility also needs an active template for account reports.

![Care screen for step 7, Open a tab](/media/billing/account/view-account-7.png)

### 8. Start a task from the account page

Use the controls on the account page to start these tasks:

| Control | Task |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | [Edit an account](./edit-account.mdx) |
| **Rebalance** | [Rebalance an account](./rebalance-account.mdx) |
| **Billing Status** stepper | [Advance the billing status](./advance-account-billing-status.mdx) |
| **Settle & Close** | [Settle and close an account](./settle-and-close-account.mdx) |
| **Add Tags** or **Manage Tags** | [Add or remove account tags](./manage-account-tags.mdx) |
| **Create Invoice** | [Create an invoice](../invoice/create-invoice.mdx) |
| **Advance/Receipt** | [Record an advance payment](../payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment.mdx) |
| **Record Credit Note** | [Record a credit note](../payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note.mdx) |
| **Transfer Payment** | [Transfer an amount to another account](../payment-reconciliation/transfer-payment.mdx) |

Note: Some controls show only for an Active account that does not have a closed Billing Status. Each task page lists its conditions.

Note: **Record Credit Note** and **Transfer Payment** are in the more-options (**⋮**) menu. On narrow screens, **Settle & Close** also moves into the more-options (**⋮**) menu. **Create Invoice** and **Advance/Receipt** then show as **Invoice** and **Credit**.

![Care screen for step 8, Start a task from the account page](/media/billing/account/view-account-8.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows the account page with the details, the totals, the **Billing Status** stepper, and the tabs of the account.
- You can start the account tasks from the account page.
