# Create an account

## Overview

<video src="/media/billing/account/create-account.mp4" poster="/media/billing/account/create-account.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/billing/account/create-account.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to create an [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) for a patient at a facility. The account collects the charge items, invoices, and payments of that patient at that facility.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You are a member of the facility.
- You can open the patient's profile in the facility. See [View a patient's profile](../../clinical/patient/view-patient-profile.mdx).
- If the new account is Active and Open, the patient has no other Active, Open account at the facility.
- You have the permissions listed below.

Note: Some actions add charge items without a chosen account, for example medication returns. If the patient then has no Active, Open account at the facility, Care creates one.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create Account | Shows **Create Account** and lets you save the new account. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin holds it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you see the patient's account list, where **Create Account** is. You must hold it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist hold it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the patient's accounts

Open the patient's profile in the facility.

Select the **Accounts** tab. Care shows the accounts of the patient at this facility.

Note: The **Accounts** tab shows only when you open the patient inside a facility.

Note: The account list in **Billing** > **Accounts** has no **Create Account** button.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the patient's accounts](/media/billing/account/create-account-1.png)

### 2. Open the account form

Select **Create Account**. Care opens the **Create Account** form.

Note: You can also start from the **Account** card of an encounter. See [Link an encounter to an account](./link-encounter-to-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 2, Open the account form](/media/billing/account/create-account-2.png)

### 3. Enter the account details

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The name of the account. This field is required. If you leave it empty, Care shows **Name is required**. |
| **Description** | More detail about the account. This field is optional. |
| **Status** | The status of the account: Active, Inactive, Entered in Error, or On Hold. The form selects Active. |
| **Billing Status** | Where the account is in billing: Open, Care Completed, or Billing. The form selects Open. |

Note: Your deployment can add more fields to the form.

Note: You do not enter the start date. Care sets it to the time when you create the account.

Note: You cannot set the primary encounter on this form. You can set it later. See [Edit an account](./edit-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 3, Enter the account details](/media/billing/account/create-account-3.png)

### 4. Save the account

Select **Create**.

Note: **Create** stays disabled until you change a field in the form.

If the new account is Active and Open, and the patient already has such an account, Care does not save the account. Care shows "Active account already exists for this patient".

![Care screen for step 4, Save the account](/media/billing/account/create-account-4.png)

![Care screen for step 4, Save the account](/media/billing/account/create-account-5.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care saves the account and closes the form. Care shows no success message.
- The account shows in the patient's account list. The list shows Active accounts when it opens. To see an account with another status, change the **Account Status** filter.
- The **Start date** of the account is the time when you created it.
- The account belongs to the patient and the facility. You cannot change the patient or the facility later.
