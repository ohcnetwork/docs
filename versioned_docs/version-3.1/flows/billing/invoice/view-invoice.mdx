# Find and open an invoice

## Overview

This flow describes how to find an [invoice](../../../concepts/billing/invoice.mdx) in Care and open its page. The invoice page shows the totals, the lines and the payments of the invoice, and the actions for its status.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of an organization of the facility that holds the invoice.
- If the invoice is locked, you have Can Manage Locked Invoice. Without it, the invoice does not open.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Invoice | List invoices and open an invoice. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |
| Can Manage Locked Invoice | Only to open a locked invoice. Facility Admin holds it. |
| Can Read Account | Only if you start from an account: open the account and its **Invoices** tab. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |
| Can Read Charge Item | Only if you start from the **Charge Items** tab of an account: list the charge items. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |
| Can Read Payment Reconciliation | Only if you start from a payment: open the payment. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |

You hold each permission through a role in any organization of the facility. The roles named are the default roles.

The pharmacy screens have their own permissions. See [Dispense a medication](../../medications/medication-dispense/dispense-medication.mdx).

## Steps

### 1. Open the invoice list

Open the list of all invoices of the facility, or the invoices of one account.

- All invoices: select **Billing**. Select **Invoices**. Care opens **Invoice Management**.
- One account: open the account. The account page opens on its **Invoices** tab.

For ways to find an account, see [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the invoice list](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-1.png)

### 2. Find the invoice

Enter the invoice number in **Search Invoices**.

On **Invoice Management**, you can also use **Filter by Patient**. The **Invoices** tab of an account does not have it.

To narrow the list, use the **Invoice Status**, **Created By** and **Invoice Date** filters.

The list shows **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date**, **Account**, **Status** and **Total** for each invoice. The account tab does not show the **Account** column.

Note: **Invoice Date** is the date when someone created the invoice. It is not the **Issue Date** of the invoice.

Note: For a locked invoice, the **Total** column shows a **Locked** badge instead of the amount. Every user sees the badge, also a Facility Admin.

If no invoice matches, Care shows **No invoices**.

![Care screen for step 2, Find the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-2.png)

### 3. Open the invoice

Select **See Invoice** on the row of the invoice.

You can also open an invoice from these places:

- The **Charge Items** tab of the account: select the invoice number on a charge item.
- The page of a payment against the invoice: select **View Invoice**.
- A pharmacy dispense order or a medication return: select **View Invoice**.

Note: The QR code on a printed invoice holds the patient's ID. **Scan Invoice QR** does not open an invoice from that code.

![Care screen for step 3, Open the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-3.png)

### 4. Read the invoice

The invoice page shows these parts.

| Part of the page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | **Patient Name**, and the **Account** with its **Amount Due** and **Status**. The amount due and the status are those of the account, not of the invoice. |
| **Invoice Summary** | The status of the invoice, **Total Amount**, **Total Payments Received** and **Balance Due**. |
| Invoice heading | The invoice number and status, and a **Locked** badge when the invoice is locked. |
| **Tax Invoice** | **Bill To** with the name, address, phone number and identifiers of the patient, the note, and the **Issue Date**. |
| Lines | Each charge item with **Item**, **Performer**, **Unit Price**, **Qty**, **Discount**, one column for each tax, and **Total**. |
| Totals | The base amounts, surcharges, discounts, **Net Amount**, taxes and **Total**. |
| **Payments Received Against This Invoice** | The active payments with **Date and Time**, **Payment Method**, **Reference** and **Amount**, and **Total Received**. |
| **Credit Notes Issued Against This Invoice** | The active credit notes and **Total Credit Notes**. |
| **Payment Terms** | The payment terms, when the invoice has them. |
| Footer | **Invoice Updated** and **Draft Invoice Created**, each with who and when. |

A refund invoice shows **Total Credit Notes**, **Refunds Given Against This Invoice** and **Total Refunded** in place of the payment labels.

If a Balanced invoice has an unpaid amount, the page shows that amount with the text "was unpaid and has been added to the account due."

![Care screen for step 4, Read the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-4.png)

![Care screen for step 4, Read the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-5.png)

![Care screen for step 4, Read the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/view-invoice-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The invoice page opens.
- If the invoice does not open, the page shows **Invoice not found**, **Invoice may not exist** and **Back to invoices**. This happens, for example, when you do not have Can Read Invoice.
- If the invoice is locked and you do not have Can Manage Locked Invoice, the page shows **Invoice not found**, **Invoice may not exist** and **Back to invoices**. Care also shows the error "Locked invoice permission denied."
- From the invoice page, you can print the invoice. Depending on its status, you can also edit it, issue it, mark it as balanced, lock it or cancel it.
