# Lock or unlock an invoice

## Overview

This flow describes how to lock or unlock an [invoice](../../../concepts/billing/invoice.mdx) in Care. A lock limits who can open the invoice and hides its total in lists, but it does not stop changes to the invoice.

## Pre-requisites

- The invoice status is Draft, Issued or Balanced. Care does not offer these actions for a Cancelled or Entered in Error invoice.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Manage Locked Invoice | Lock and unlock the invoice, and open it while it is locked. Facility Admin holds it. |
| Can Read Invoice | Open the invoice. Care also checks it when you lock or unlock the invoice. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |

You hold each permission through a role in any organization of the facility. The roles named are the default roles.

Care shows **Lock Invoice** to every user who can open the invoice. If you do not have Can Manage Locked Invoice, Care shows "Locked invoice permission denied." and **Failed to lock invoice**.

## Steps

### 1. Open the invoice

Open the invoice. See [Find and open an invoice](./view-invoice.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/lock-or-unlock-invoice-1.png)

### 2. Open the menu

Open the more-options (**⋮**) menu.

![Care screen for step 2, Open the menu](/media/billing/invoice/lock-or-unlock-invoice-2.png)

### 3. Lock or unlock the invoice

To lock the invoice, select **Lock Invoice**.

To unlock a locked invoice, select **Unlock Invoice**.

Care applies the change at once. Care does not ask you to confirm.

![Care screen for step 3, Lock or unlock the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/lock-or-unlock-invoice-3.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Lock or unlock the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/lock-or-unlock-invoice-4.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Lock or unlock the invoice](/media/billing/invoice/lock-or-unlock-invoice-5.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows **Invoice locked successfully** or **Invoice unlocked successfully**.
- A locked invoice shows a **Locked** badge in its heading.
- In the **Invoices** tab of the account and in **Billing** > **Invoices**, the **Total** column of a locked invoice shows a **Locked** badge instead of the amount. Every user sees the badge, also a Facility Admin.
- Users without Can Manage Locked Invoice cannot open a locked invoice. For them, the invoice page shows **Invoice not found**, **Invoice may not exist** and **Back to invoices**. Care also shows the error "Locked invoice permission denied."
- The lock does not change the status of the invoice. A locked invoice can still be edited, issued, marked as balanced or cancelled.
- Care records who locked or unlocked the invoice, and when. No screen shows this record.
- Care does not print the charge items of an account while one of them is on a locked invoice. This also applies to users with Can Manage Locked Invoice. See [Print charge items](../charge-item/print-charge-items.mdx).
