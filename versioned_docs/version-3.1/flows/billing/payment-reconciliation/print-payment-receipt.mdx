# Print a payment receipt

## Overview

This flow describes how to print the receipt of a [payment](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) or a credit note in Care.

Care builds the receipt as a PDF from the details of the payment.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- Someone recorded the payment in this facility.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Payment Reconciliation | Lets you open the payment and its receipt. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Needed only if you start from the invoice page. Lets you open the invoice. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the receipt

Start from one of these places:

- On the payment page, select **Print Receipt**, or press `p`.
- For a payment against an invoice, select **Print** in its row on the invoice page. The table is "Payments Received Against This Invoice", or "Refunds Given Against This Invoice" on a refund invoice.

To open the payment page, see [View a payment](./view-payment.mdx).

Care shows a preview with the title "Payment Receipt", or "Refund Receipt" for a credit note.

Note: After you record a payment against an invoice, the form also shows **Print Receipt**. That button opens the print page of the invoice, not this receipt.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the receipt](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/print-payment-receipt-1.png)

![Care screen for step 1, Open the receipt](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/print-payment-receipt-2.png)

### 2. Print the receipt

Select **Print**, or press `p`.

Note: The button can read **Share** in place of **Print**.

To leave the preview without printing, select **Back**, or press `Escape`.

Note: If the receipt template of the facility has auto-print turned on, the print dialog opens by itself.

![Care screen for step 2, Print the receipt](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/print-payment-receipt-3.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The receipt shows the name, age, sex and address of the patient, and the official identifiers of the patient.
- The receipt shows the Date, the Payment ID and the payment method.
- If the payment is against an invoice, the receipt shows the invoice number, status and amount.
- The receipt shows the type, kind, issuer type and amount, the total, the notes and the location. For cash, it also shows the amount tendered and the amount returned.
- The Date on the receipt is the date when the payment was entered in Care, not the Payment Date.
- "Generated by" on the receipt names the person who last updated the payment.
- A Cancelled or Entered in Error payment has a red "Cancelled" or "Entered in Error" watermark on the receipt.
- The receipt uses the receipt template settings of the facility.
