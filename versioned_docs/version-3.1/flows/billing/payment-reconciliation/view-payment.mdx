# View a payment

## Overview

This flow describes how to open a [payment](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) in Care and read its details.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- Someone recorded the payment in this facility.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Payment Reconciliation | Lets you open the payment. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility. Without it, Care shows "Payment Not Found". Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Needed only if you start from the invoice page, or to open the invoice with **View Invoice**. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Needed only if you start from the **Payments** tab of the account, or to open the account with **View Account**. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the payment

Start from one of these places:

- In the facility, select **Billing**. Select **Payments**. In the row of the payment, select **View**.
- On the account page, select the **Payments** tab. In the row of the payment, select **View**.
- On the invoice page, under "Payments Received Against This Invoice", select **View** for the payment.

To filter the payments list, see [Find payments](./find-payments.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the payment](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-1.png)

### 2. Read the header

The heading shows "Payment", or "Refund" for a credit note, and the Payment ID.

Badges show the status, the outcome, the method and the type of the payment.

![Care screen for step 2, Read the header](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-2.png)

### 3. Read the payment details

The page shows these sections.

| Component | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Patient Information | The Patient Name, Age and Sex. |
| Payment Amount | The amount and the Payment Date. For a credit note, the label is Refund Amount. |
| Payment Details | The Payment Method, Reconciliation Type, Kind and Issuer Type. It also shows the Reference Number and the Location, if the payment has them. |
| Cash Transaction Details | Only for cash. The Amount Tendered and, for a payment, the Change Returned. |
| Notes | The notes of the payment, if it has notes. |
| Related Invoice | Only for a payment against an invoice. The invoice number, its status, the Invoice Amount, **View Invoice** and **Print Invoice**. |
| Payment Timeline | "Payment Recorded" at the payment date. For a cancelled payment, also "Payment Cancelled". |
| Created By and Last Modified By | Who recorded the payment and who changed it last, each with a date. |

Note: The time next to "Payment Cancelled" is the current time, not the time of the cancellation.

![Care screen for step 3, Read the payment details](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-3.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Read the payment details](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-4.png)

### 4. Open the invoice or the account

If the payment is against an invoice, select **View Invoice** to open the invoice. You can also press `v`.

To open the account, select **View Account**. You can also press `a`.

To go back to the list, select **Back to Payments**.

Note: To print the receipt, select **Print Receipt**. See [Print a payment receipt](./print-payment-receipt.mdx).

![Care screen for step 4, Open the invoice or the account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-5.png)

![Care screen for step 4, Open the invoice or the account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/view-payment-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows the details of the payment.
- The page also shows **Mark as Cancelled** and **Mark as entered in error**. It hides them once the payment is Cancelled or Entered in Error. See [Cancel a payment](./cancel-payment.mdx).
