# Record an advance payment

## Overview

This flow describes how to record an advance [payment](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) on an [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) in Care. Care records the payment to the account, not to an invoice, and it counts towards the Total Paid of the account.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- The account has the status Active.
- The billing status of the account is not Closed Bad Debt, Closed Voided, Closed Completed or Closed Combined.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Write Payment Reconciliation | Lets you save the payment. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility. Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Lets you see locations in the location picker and save the payment with a location. You need it in an organization that the location is linked to. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you find and open the account. You need it in the facility. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the invoices on the **Invoices** tab, where the account page opens. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |

Care shows **Advance/Receipt** without a permission check. If you do not have Can Write Payment Reconciliation, Care shows an error when you save.

## Steps

### 1. Open the account

In the facility, select **Billing**. Select **Accounts**.

In the row of the account, select **Go to account**.

Note: You can also open the account from the **Accounts** tab of the patient. See [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-1.png)

### 2. Open the payment form

Select **Advance/Receipt**.

On a narrow screen, select **Credit**.

Note: Press `c` on a wide screen, or `r` on a narrow screen, to open the payment form.

The form "Collect Payment" opens. It shows the Balance Due of the account.

![Care screen for step 2, Open the payment form](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-2.png)

### 3. Enter the payment details

Complete these fields.

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Payment Method | How the payer pays. Select Cash, Direct Deposit, Credit Card, Debit Card, Check or Credit Account. |
| Payment Type | Care selects Advance. You can select Payment instead. |
| Issuer Type | Who pays. Care selects Patient. You can select Insurer instead. |
| Location | A location of the facility. Select it in the location picker. Care fills in the location that you used last in this facility, on this browser. |
| Amount Paid | The amount of this payment. The amount must be more than 0. |
| Amount Received | Only for Cash. The cash that the payer gives you. It cannot be less than the Amount Paid. |
| Reference Number | Only for methods other than Cash. Optional. A transaction ID, check number or other reference. |
| Payment Date | The date and time of the payment. Care fills in the current time. It cannot be in the future. |
| Notes | Optional. More information about the payment. |

If the cash that the payer gives is more than the Amount Paid, Care shows the "Change to return".

Note: Your deployment decides if **Location** is required. By default, it is required.

Note: Your deployment can preselect a **Payment Method**.

![Care screen for step 3, Enter the payment details](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-3.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Enter the payment details](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-4.png)

### 4. Save the payment

Select **Record Payment**.

Note: Press `Shift+Enter` to save. To close the form without saving, select **Cancel** or press `Escape`.

Note: **Record Payment** stays disabled until the amount is more than 0.

Note: If Care shows "You do not have permission to given location", select another location. You need Can List Facility Locations in an organization of the location.

Care shows "Payment Recorded Successfully!" and "The payment has been recorded to the account."

![Care screen for step 4, Save the payment](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-5.png)

### 5. Close the form

Select **Close**.

![Care screen for step 5, Close the form](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-6.png)

![Care screen for step 5, Close the form](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-advance-payment-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care saves the payment on the account, without an invoice.
- Care calculates the totals of the account again at once. The Total Paid goes up and the Amount Due goes down.
- The payment shows on the **Payments** tab of the account.
- You can print a receipt from the page of the payment. See [Print a payment receipt](./print-payment-receipt.mdx).
