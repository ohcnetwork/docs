# Cancel a payment

## Overview

This flow describes how to cancel a [payment](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) in Care, or mark it entered in error.

After you confirm, the payment no longer counts towards the totals of its [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx), and Care calculates the totals again at once.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- The status of the payment is not Cancelled or Entered in Error.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Destroy Payment Reconciliation | Lets you cancel a payment or mark it entered in error. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility of the payment. Facility Admin has it by default. |
| Can Write Payment Reconciliation | Lets you cancel a payment within a set period after it is recorded, if your deployment sets a period. By default the period is 0 minutes, so you need Can Destroy Payment Reconciliation. Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Payment Reconciliation | Lets you open the payment. Care also needs it when you confirm the cancellation. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |

Care shows the cancel buttons without a permission check. If you do not have Can Destroy Payment Reconciliation, Care shows "User does not have permission to cancel payment reconciliation" when you confirm. Within a period set by your deployment, Can Write Payment Reconciliation is enough.

## Steps

### 1. Open the payment

Open the page of the payment that you want to cancel. See [View a payment](./view-payment.mdx).

![Care screen for step 1, Open the payment](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/cancel-payment-1.png)

### 2. Choose the action

Select one of these actions:

| Action | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark as Cancelled** | The status of the payment changes to Cancelled. Press `c` to select it. |
| **Mark as entered in error** | The status of the payment changes to Entered in Error. This status means that someone recorded the payment by mistake. Press `e` to select it. |

![Care screen for step 2, Choose the action](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/cancel-payment-2.png)

### 3. Confirm the action

For **Mark as Cancelled**, type "Cancel Payment" in the dialog "Confirm Cancel Payment".

For **Mark as entered in error**, type "Mark as Error" in the dialog "Confirm Mark as Entered in Error".

Warning: You cannot undo this action.

Select **Proceed**.

Note: **Proceed** is available only when the text that you type matches exactly.

![Care screen for step 3, Confirm the action](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/cancel-payment-3.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Confirm the action](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/cancel-payment-4.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows "Payment status updated".
- The payment shows the status Cancelled or Entered in Error.
- The payment no longer counts towards the totals of the account. Care calculates the totals again at once.
- The page no longer shows **Mark as Cancelled** and **Mark as entered in error**.
- If the payment was against an invoice, the invoice keeps its status. A Balanced invoice stays Balanced.
- The receipt of the payment shows a red "Cancelled" or "Entered in Error" watermark.
