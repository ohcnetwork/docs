# Move payments to another account

## Overview

This flow describes how to move [payments](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) from one [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) to one of the patient's other accounts with **Change Account**.

[Transfer an amount to another account](./transfer-payment.mdx) does not move existing payments: it records two new payments for an amount that you type.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- The status of each payment is Active or Draft.
- No payment that you move is recorded against an invoice.
- The patient has another account in this facility.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Write Payment Reconciliation | Lets you select payments and move them. Without it, the **Payments** tab shows no checkboxes. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility. Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Payment Reconciliation | Lets you see the payments on the **Payments** tab. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Lets you open the account and see the patient's other accounts in the target account list. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you see the invoices on the **Invoices** tab, where the account page opens. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the Payments tab of the account

Open the account that holds the payments. See [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx).

Select the **Payments** tab, or press `Shift+3`.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the Payments tab of the account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-1.png)

### 2. Select the payments

Select the checkbox of each payment that you want to move.

To select all the payments on this page that you can move, select the checkbox in the header.

Note: A payment against an invoice, or a payment that is not Active or Draft, has a disabled checkbox. Hover over the checkbox to see the reason.

![Care screen for step 2, Select the payments](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-2.png)

### 3. Open Change Account

In the bar that shows the number of selected items, select **Change Account**.

Note: To clear the selection, select **Clear Selection**.

The form "Change Account" opens. It lists the selected payments.

![Care screen for step 3, Open Change Account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-3.png)

### 4. Select the target account

Under "Select target account", select the account that receives the payments.

The list shows the patient's other accounts in this facility, of every status, each with its billing status.

Note: If the patient has no other account, Care shows "No other accounts available".

![Care screen for step 4, Select the target account](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-4.png)

### 5. Move the payments

Select **Change Account**.

Note: If another change to the account is in progress, Care does not move the payments and shows an error.

![Care screen for step 5, Move the payments](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-5.png)

![Care screen for step 5, Move the payments](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/move-payment-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows "Payments moved to new account successfully".
- The payments leave the **Payments** tab of this account and show on the target account.
- Care calculates the totals of both accounts again.
- Care moves all the selected payments, or none of them.
