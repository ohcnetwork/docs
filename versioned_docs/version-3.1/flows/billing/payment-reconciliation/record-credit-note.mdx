# Record a credit note

## Overview

This flow describes how to record a credit note, a [payment](../../../concepts/billing/payment-reconciliation.mdx) that records a refund, from an [account](../../../concepts/billing/account.mdx) or a refund [invoice](../../../concepts/billing/invoice.mdx). Care subtracts the credit note from the Total Paid of the account.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- If you start from the account, the account has the status Active.
- If you start from the account, its billing status is not closed. The closed statuses are Closed Bad Debt, Closed Voided, Closed Completed and Closed Combined.
- If you start from an invoice, it is a refund invoice with the status Issued. Care creates refund invoices for returned items.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Write Payment Reconciliation | Lets you save the credit note. You need it through a role in any organization of the facility. Facility Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Lets you see locations in the location picker and save the credit note with a location. You need it in an organization that the location is linked to. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Account | Needed if you start from the account. Lets you find and open the account. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Read Invoice | Lets you open the refund invoice, and see the **Invoices** tab, where the account page opens. Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist have it by default. |
| Can Manage Locked Invoice | Needed only for a locked invoice. Lets you open the locked invoice. Facility Admin has it by default. |

Care shows **Record Credit Note** without a permission check. If you do not have Can Write Payment Reconciliation, Care shows an error when you save.

## Steps

### 1. Open the credit note form

Start from one of these places:

- On the account page, open the more-options (**⋮**) menu. Select **Record Credit Note**.
- On the refund invoice, select **Record Credit Note**.

To open the account, see [View an account](../account/view-account.mdx). To open the invoice, see [Find and open an invoice](../invoice/view-invoice.mdx).

The form "Record Credit Note" opens.

From a refund invoice, the form shows the Invoice Total and the Refund Given. From the account, it shows the Balance Due of the account.

![Care screen for step 1, Open the credit note form](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note-1.png)

![Care screen for step 1, Open the credit note form](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note-2.png)

### 2. Enter the credit note details

Complete these fields.

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Payment Method | The method of the refund. Select Cash, Direct Deposit, Credit Card, Debit Card, Check or Credit Account. |
| Payment Type | The form has no preselected type for a credit note. Select Payment or Advance. |
| Location | A location of the facility. Select it in the location picker. Care fills in the location that you used last in this facility, on this browser. |
| Refund Amount | The amount of the refund. From a refund invoice, Care fills in the invoice total. The amount must be more than 0. |
| Reference Number | Only for methods other than Cash. Optional. A transaction ID, check number or other reference. |
| Payment Date | The date and time of the refund. Care fills in the current time. It cannot be in the future. |
| Notes | Optional. More information about the refund. |

The form does not ask for an Issuer Type or an Amount Received. Care saves the issuer as Patient.

Note: Your deployment decides if **Location** is required. By default, it is required.

Note: Your deployment can preselect a **Payment Method**.

![Care screen for step 2, Enter the credit note details](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note-3.png)

### 3. Save the credit note

Select **Record Credit Note**.

Note: Press `Shift+Enter` to save. To close the form without saving, select **Cancel** or press `Escape`.

Note: **Record Credit Note** stays disabled until the amount is more than 0.

Note: If Care shows "You do not have permission to given location", select another location. You need Can List Facility Locations in an organization of the location.

Care shows "Credit Note Recorded Successfully!"

![Care screen for step 3, Save the credit note](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note-4.png)

![Care screen for step 3, Save the credit note](/media/billing/payment-reconciliation/record-credit-note-5.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care saves the credit note on the account. If you started from a refund invoice, Care also records it against that invoice.
- Care subtracts the credit note from the Total Paid of the account at once. The Amount Due goes up.
- Payment lists show the credit note as a negative amount.
- If you started from a refund invoice, its page lists the credit note under "Credit Notes Issued Against This Invoice".
- Care does not mark the refund invoice Balanced by itself.
