# Move a token in the queue

## Overview

<video src="/media/scheduling/token/move-token.mp4" poster="/media/scheduling/token/move-token.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/scheduling/token/move-token.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to move a [token](../../../concepts/scheduling/token.mdx) on the queue board. You can:

- Serve or call a waiting token out of order.
- Move a token to another service point.
- Move a served token back to calling.
- Set a token aside for re-call, and bring it back.
- Move a token back to **Waiting**.
- Bring back a completed token from the **Finished** tab.

To serve tokens in the usual order, see [Serve patients from a token queue](./manage-token-queue.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- You open the queue board of the queue that holds the token. See [Serve patients from a token queue](./manage-token-queue.mdx).
- If you move a token to a service point, that service point has a section on the board.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create on Token | Lets you move a token between **Waiting**, the service points and **Awaiting Re-Call**, and back from the **Finished** tab. Hold it in the place of the queue's resource, as the list below the table shows. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can list token on Object | Lets you open the queue board and see its tokens, its service points and the **Finished** tab. Hold it in the same place as "Can Create on Token". By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can list bookings on Object | Shows **Queues** under **Patient Management** in the facility sidebar, where you reach the queues of a practitioner. Hold it in the Administration organization, which every facility has, or in a department or team of the facility. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can Read Healthcare Service | Lets you reach the queues of a healthcare service, or of a location, through **Services** in the facility sidebar. Hold it in any organization of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold it. |
| Can View Facility Organizations | Needed only to choose a practitioner outside your own departments, under **All Dept.** in **Selected practitioner**. Hold it in any organization of the facility. The Administration organization appears in the list only if you hold it there. By default, the Facility Admin, Admin, Staff, Doctor, Administrator, Nurse and Volunteer roles hold it. |

Where you must hold "Can Create on Token" and "Can list token on Object" depends on the resource of the queue:

- For a practitioner's queue: the Administration organization, or an organization of the facility that the practitioner belongs to, or a parent of that organization.
- For a location's queue: an organization linked to the location or to its parent location, or a parent of one of them. The Administration organization also counts.
- For a healthcare service's queue: the service's managing organization or a parent of it. If the service has no managing organization, the Administration organization.
- If a department or team manages the service, hold them in that department or team, or in one above it. A role in the Administration organization alone does not count.

Note: The queue board shows its actions to everyone who can open it. If you do not hold "Can Create on Token" in the right place, Care refuses the change.

## Steps

### 1. Open the menu of the token

On the **Ongoing** tab, find the token.

Select the three-dot button on the token card, or right-click the card.

The menu shows only the moves that fit the place and the label of the token.

Each move in this menu applies at once, with no confirmation.

![The menu of the waiting token of Fathima Rasheed with the Mark as In-service to and Call to items for each service point](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-1.png)

### 2. Serve or call a waiting token out of order

This step applies to a token in **Waiting**.

Each service point with a section on the board has a "Mark as In-service to [service point name]" item.

Each of these service points also has a "Call to [service point name]" item.

To serve the patient now, select "Mark as In-service to" with the name of your service point.

The token moves under **Now Serving** at that service point, with the label **Serving**.

To call the patient without serving yet, select "Call to" with the name of the service point.

The token moves to the section of that service point, below **Now Serving**, with the label **Call Next**.

To serve a called token, see [Serve patients from a token queue](./manage-token-queue.mdx).

![The Room 1 section with Fathima Rasheed served out of order, labelled Serving with the token REV-001](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-2.png)

### 3. Move a token to another service point

This step applies to a token in the section of a service point.

To serve the patient at another service point, select "Reassign to [service point name]" for that service point.

The token moves under **Now Serving** at that service point.

To call the patient to another service point, select "Call to [service point name]" for that service point. The token gets the label **Call Next**.

The menu does not offer the service point that has the token now.

A service point records the token that it took with **Serve Next Patient**, until that token leaves the service point.

Care refuses to move that token to another service point.

To move such a token, select **Move to Waiting** first. Then call it to the other service point.

![The Room 2 section with Fathima Rasheed after Reassign to Room 2, labelled Serving](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-3.png)

### 4. Move a served token back to calling

This step applies to a token with the label **Serving**.

Select **Move to Calling**.

The token stays at the same service point, with the label **Call Next**.

### 5. Set a token aside for re-call

This step applies to a token with the label **Serving**.

Select **Move to Awaiting Recall**.

The token leaves its service point and gets the label **Recall**. You find it under **Awaiting Re-Call**.

![The Awaiting Re-Call button outlined with a count of 1](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-4.png)

### 6. Bring back a token from Awaiting Re-Call

Select **Awaiting Re-Call** in the header of the **Called + Now Serving** column.

If no token waits for re-call, the button is not available.

The dialog lists the tokens that wait for re-call.

Open the menu of the token on its card in the dialog.

To serve the patient now, select "Mark as In-service to" with the name of your service point.

To call the patient first, select "Call to" with the name of the service point.

After "Call to", the dialog stays open. Close the dialog to go back to the board.

![The Awaiting Re-Call dialog with the token menu of Fathima Rasheed and Call to Room 1 outlined](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-5.png)

![The Room 1 section with Fathima Rasheed labelled Call Next below Now Serving](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-6.png)

### 7. Move a token back to Waiting

This step applies to a token in the section of a service point.

Select **Move to Waiting**.

The token leaves its service point and goes back to the **Waiting** column.

![The Waiting column with Fathima Rasheed back in Waiting](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-7.png)

### 8. Bring back a completed token

Select the **Finished** tab.

Find the token with the status **Fulfilled**.

Select the three-dot button in the row of the token.

If the token had a service point, select **Move back to In-service**.

If the token had no service point, select **Move back to waiting**.

A dialog asks you to confirm, with the token number and the patient's name.

Select **Confirm**.

**Move back to In-service** puts the token under **Now Serving** at its service point.

**Move back to waiting** puts the token in the **Waiting** column.

To set the token aside for re-call instead, select **Move to Awaiting Recall**. It applies at once.

![The confirmation dialog to move the token GEN-002 of Rahul Menon back to in service, with Cancel and Confirm](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-8.png)

![The Room 1 section with Rahul Menon labelled Serving with the token GEN-002 after the move back](/media/scheduling/token/move-token-9.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The token is in its new place on the **Ongoing** tab. Its label shows its new state.
- A token that you bring back from the **Finished** tab leaves that tab.
