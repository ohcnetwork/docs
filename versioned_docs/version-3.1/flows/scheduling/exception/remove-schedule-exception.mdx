# Remove a schedule exception

## Overview

<video src="/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception.mp4" poster="/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to remove a [schedule exception](../../../concepts/scheduling/exception.mdx) in Care. Use it when the practitioner, location or healthcare service is available again for that period.

## Pre-requisites

- You are a member of the facility.
- The exception is recorded for the practitioner, location or healthcare service. See [Add a schedule exception](./create-schedule-exception.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create on Schedule | Lets you remove the exception. You must hold it in the place given below for the resource. By default, the Admin (facility role), Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse roles hold it. |
| Can list schedule on Object | Lets you open the schedule page and see its exceptions. Care checks it in the same place as Can Create on Schedule. For a practitioner, Care shows the **Availability** tab only if you hold it in a department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can Read Healthcare Service | Lets you open **Services** and a service, on the way to the schedule of a location or a healthcare service. A role in any organization of the facility counts. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Admin, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold it. |

Care accepts Can Create on Schedule and Can list schedule on Object only in these places:

- For a practitioner: the facility's Administration organization, a department or team that the practitioner belongs to, or a parent of it.
- For a location: a department linked to the location or to its parent location, a parent of that department, or the facility's Administration organization.
- For a healthcare service with a managing organization: that organization or a parent of it. The Administration organization does not count.
- For a healthcare service without a managing organization: only the facility's Administration organization.

Only roles that you hold in the facility's organizations count. A role in a governance organization above the facility does not count.

Care shows **Remove** on every exception to every user who can open the **Exceptions** tab. Care removes the exception only if you hold Can Create on Schedule in the place given above. For example, by default the Administrator role can see exceptions but cannot remove them.

## Steps

### 1. Open the schedule

Each practitioner, location and healthcare service has its own schedule page. Open it in one of these ways:

- For a practitioner: in the facility sidebar, select **Users**. On the user's card, select **See Details**. Then select the **Availability** tab.
- For your own schedule: open the account menu and select **Profile**. Then select the **Availability** tab.
- For a location: in the facility sidebar, select **Services**, then a service. On a location card, select **View appointments**. This link shows for services that are not a store, a pharmacy or a lab. In the location sidebar, under **Scheduling**, select **Schedule**.
- For a healthcare service: in the facility sidebar, select **Services**, then the service. In the service sidebar, under **Scheduling**, select **Schedule**.

![The account menu open at the bottom of the facility sidebar, with Profile outlined under Dr. Meera Iyer](/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception-1.png)

Care can refuse to load the page and show "You do not have permission to update schedule". This means that you do not hold Can list schedule on Object in the place for this resource. Ask your administrator for it.

The page opens on the **Schedule** tab, next to the **Exceptions** tab.

### 2. Find the exception

Select the **Exceptions** tab. The tab lists the exceptions that overlap the month shown on the calendar.

![The Training session exception, 2 PM - 4 PM on Sat, 28 Nov 2026, outlined at the top of the Exceptions tab](/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception-2.png)

### 3. Start the removal

On the exception that you want to remove, select **Remove**.

![The Remove button outlined on the Training session exception](/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception-3.png)

### 4. Confirm the removal

Care asks "Are you sure?" and warns "This will permanently remove the exception and cannot be undone".

![The Are you sure? dialog outlined, with the warning This will permanently remove the exception and cannot be undone, and the Cancel and Delete buttons](/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception-4.png)

Select **Delete**. Care shows "Exception deleted".

![The message Exception deleted, with the list of exceptions outlined: only Department meeting and Outreach clinic remain](/media/scheduling/exception/remove-schedule-exception-5.png)

If Care refuses with "You do not have permission to update schedule", ask your administrator for help. You need Can Create on Schedule in the place given under Permissions.

## Expected Outcome

- Care removes the exception.
- Removing the exception does not bring back the slots by itself. When someone opens the slots of a day to book an appointment, Care makes the missing slots again.
- Care does not make slots again for times that have already ended.
- Slots come back only on the days and times that a schedule template of the resource covers.
- To correct an exception, add a new one after you remove the old one. Care does not let you edit an exception.
