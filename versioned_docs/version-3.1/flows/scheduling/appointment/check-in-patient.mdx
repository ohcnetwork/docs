# Check in a patient for an appointment

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/scheduling/appointment/check-in-patient.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to check in a patient for an [appointment](../../../concepts/scheduling/booking.mdx) from **Appointment Details**. Checking in changes the appointment's status from Booked to Checked-In.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has an appointment at the facility, and its status is Booked.
- The time is between one day before and one day after the start of the appointment's slot.
- You have the permissions listed below, in the place that the list below the table gives.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can update bookings on Object | Lets you check in the patient. Hold it in the place listed below for the appointment's schedule. By default, the Admin (facility role), Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse roles hold it. Administrator does not. |
| Can list bookings on Object | Lets you open **Appointment Details**. Hold it in the place listed below for the appointment's schedule. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |

Care accepts Can update bookings on Object and Can list bookings on Object only in these places:

- For a practitioner: the facility's Administration organization, a department or team that the practitioner belongs to, or a parent of it.
- For a location: a department linked to the location or to its parent location, a parent of that department, or the facility's Administration organization.
- For a healthcare service with a managing organization: that organization or a parent of it. The Administration organization does not count.
- For a healthcare service without a managing organization: only the facility's Administration organization.

Only roles that you hold in the facility's organizations count. A role in a governance organization above the facility does not count.

Care shows **Check-In** if you hold Can update bookings on Object anywhere in the facility. This includes any department or team of the facility, and its Administration organization. When you check in, Care accepts the permission only in the place for the appointment's schedule. If Care refuses, ask your administrator for the permission in that place.

## Steps

### 1. Open the appointment

Open the appointment from the Appointments board or list, from the patient's appointments, or from the encounter page. Care shows **Appointment Details**. To find and open an appointment, see [Find and open appointments](./find-appointments.mdx).

![The Appointments board with Maya Raman's 09:30 appointment outlined in the Booked column](/media/scheduling/appointment/check-in-patient-1.png)

### 2. Check in the patient

In the patient card at the top of **Appointment Details**, select **Check-In**. Keyboard shortcut: `c`.

![The Check-In button outlined in the patient card at the top of Appointment Details](/media/scheduling/appointment/check-in-patient-2.png)

Care sets the appointment's status to Checked-In.

![The Schedule Information card outlined, showing the status Checked-In; the Check-In button is gone](/media/scheduling/appointment/check-in-patient-3.png)

Note: Care shows **Check-In** only while the status is Booked. Outside the time in the pre-requisites, the button is disabled.

## Expected Outcome

- The appointment's status is Checked-In. The **Schedule Information** card shows the status.
- **Check-In** is no longer shown, because the status is no longer Booked.
- On the Appointments board, the appointment is in the **Checked-In** column.
- You can now start the consultation. See [Start a consultation from an appointment](./start-consultation.mdx).
