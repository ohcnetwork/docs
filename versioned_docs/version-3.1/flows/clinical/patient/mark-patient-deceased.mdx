# Mark a patient as deceased

## Overview

This flow describes how to record in Care that a [patient](../../../concepts/clinical/patient) has died. You tick **Mark as deceased** on the **Update Patient Details** form, and enter the date and time of death. You can also record the death with a form that has a **Time of Death** question, about an encounter or about the patient.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You can update the patient's details. [Update a patient's details](./update-patient-details.mdx) describes how you get access to the patient.
- You know the date and time of death.
- If you use a form about an encounter, the patient has an open encounter. Its **Forms** list offers a form with a **Time of Death** question.
- If you use a form about the patient, **Add Forms** offers a form with a **Time of Death** question.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Steps

### 1. Open the update form

Open the patient's profile inside the facility. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).

On the **Demography** tab, go to the **General Info** section.

Select **Edit**. Care opens the **Update Patient Details** form.

![The General Info section of the patient's profile with the Edit button outlined](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-1.png)

### 2. Find the Deceased Status box

Go to the **Additional Details** section of the form.

If the section is closed, open it. On some deployments, the section is closed when the form opens.

Find the **Deceased Status** box.

![The Additional Details section of the Update Patient Details form, with the Deceased Status box outlined and Mark as deceased not ticked](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-2.png)

### 3. Mark the patient as deceased

Tick **Mark as deceased**.

Below **Mark as deceased**, Care shows the text "Use only if the patient has passed away before registration."

The **Register New Patient** form has the same **Deceased Status** box. See [Register a patient](./register-patient.mdx).

Care shows the **Date and Time of Death** field.

![The Deceased Status box outlined, with Mark as deceased ticked and the empty Date and Time of Death field](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-3.png)

### 4. Enter the date and time of death

Enter the date and time of death in **Date and Time of Death**.

Care marks the patient as deceased only when this field has a value.

Keep these rules in mind:

- The picker does not offer a time later than now.
- The time of death must be after the date of birth.
- If the form has an age instead of a date of birth, Care uses today minus the age.
- If the time of death is too early, Care shows "Death date must be after date of birth".

:::warning
Check the date and time of death before you select **Update**. If you later untick **Mark as deceased** and save, the patient stays deceased.
:::

![The Date and Time of Death field outlined, with a date and time entered](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-4.png)

### 5. Save the change

Select **Update**. You can also press Shift+Enter.

When you save, Care checks the time of death again. Care does not save a time of death in the future.

Care also does not save a time of death before the date of birth. If the patient has an age instead, the year of death cannot be before the year of birth.

![The Update button outlined at the end of the form, with Mark as deceased ticked and the time of death entered](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-5.png)

![The profile after saving, with the red banner outlined, showing the Deceased badge and the date and time of death](/media/clinical/patient/mark-patient-deceased-6.png)

### Another way: record the death with a form

Use this way instead of steps 1 to 5.

Use this way if one of your forms has a **Time of Death** question. Care records the death when you answer that question and save the form.

The form can be about an encounter or about the patient. Each kind opens from a different place.

Note: In the form editor, the author sets the question's **Answer type** to **Structured**, and then selects **Time of Death**.

For a form about an encounter:

1. Open the patient's profile inside the facility. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).
2. Select the **Encounters** tab.
3. On the card of the open encounter, select **View Encounter**. Care opens the **Overview** tab of the encounter.
4. On the **Overview** tab, select **Forms**. Care shows **Forms** only for an open encounter.
5. Select the form with the **Time of Death** question. Care opens the form.
6. Find the question. Its text is the text that the author of the form wrote. A required question has a red asterisk.
7. Enter the date and time of death. The picker does not offer a time later than now.
8. Select **Save Changes**.

The **Forms** list shows up to 15 active forms about an encounter, from your facility or from no facility. It shows only the forms for which you hold Can Read Questionnaires.

For a form about the patient:

1. Open the patient's profile. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).
2. Select the **Updates** tab.
3. Select **Add Patient Updates**. Care opens a page for forms about the patient.
4. Use **Add Forms** to select the form with the **Time of Death** question.
5. Answer the question as in steps 6 and 7 above. Then select **Save Changes**.

**Add Forms** offers only active forms about a patient. It shows only the forms for which you hold Can Read Questionnaires.

If you leave a required question empty, Care shows "This field is required".

If the question is not required and you leave it empty, Care does not record the death.

When you save, Care saves the time of death and marks the patient as deceased.

Care saves the time of death and the other answers together. If Care refuses the time of death, it saves none of the answers.

## Expected Outcome

- If you used the update form, Care shows "Patient Updated Successfully", and returns you to the previous page.
- The header of the patient's profile shows a red banner with the **Deceased** badge.
- The banner shows "Passed away on", then the date and the time of death. The date shows the month, the day and the year. The time shows hours and minutes, with AM or PM.
- When you open the update form again, **Mark as deceased** is ticked.
