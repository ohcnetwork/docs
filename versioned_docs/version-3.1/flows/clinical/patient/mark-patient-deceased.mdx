# Mark a patient as deceased

## Overview

This flow describes how to record that a [patient](../../../concepts/clinical/patient) is deceased. You record the status and the date and time of death from the patient's profile.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You can open the patient's profile.
- You have the permission listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update a Patient's data | Lets you change the patient's details, and mark the patient as deceased. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the patient's profile

Open the profile of the patient. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).

### 2. Edit the additional details

Find the **Additional Details** section. Select **Edit**.

### 3. Mark the patient as deceased

Select the **Patient is deceased** checkbox. Care shows a field for the date and time of death.

### 4. Enter the date and time of death

Enter the date and time of death.

Note: The date and time of death must not be in the future.

Note: The date and time of death must not be earlier than the patient's date of birth. If the patient has an age instead of a date of birth, Care compares the date of death with the birth year that it calculates from the age.

### 5. Save the change

Save the **Additional Details** section. See [Update patient details](./update-patient-details.mdx).

## Expected Outcome

- The patient's profile shows the deceased status as "Passed away on `{date}`, at `{time}`".
