# View a patient's profile

This flow walks through opening the profile of a **patient** in Care. The profile shows the patient's demographic, contact, and clinical information in tabs.

:::info Prerequisite
Read [Patient](../../../concepts/clinical/patient) to understand what a patient record contains.
:::

## Before you start

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You find the patient through a search, or you open the patient from an Encounter, an Appointment, or another clinical record of that patient.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Open the patient's profile and the Clinical History tab. |
| Can list patients | See the Encounters tab. A permission to list encounters also gives this access. |
| Can view questionnaire responses on patient | See the Questionnaire Updates tab. |
| Permission to view appointments | See the Appointments tab. |

Some deployments relax the check for clinical data — your access to the profile can depend on your deployment's configuration.

## Steps

### 1. Open the patient

Find the patient using [Search for a patient](./search-patient.mdx), then select the patient in the search results. Care opens the patient's profile.

You can also open the profile from a patient's Encounter, Appointment, or other clinical record.

### 2. Read the patient's information

The profile shows the patient's information in tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| General Info | The patient's core demographic and contact details. |
| Additional Details | The address, geo-organization, blood group, deceased status, and similar information. |
| Identifiers | The identifiers configured for the patient. |
| Patient Tags | The labels attached to the patient record. |
| Insurance Details | The patient's insurance information. |
| Social Profile | The patient's social information. |
| Volunteer Contact | The users assigned to support this patient in the field, for example volunteers. |

### 3. Open the other tabs

Care shows more tabs when you have the necessary permissions:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Encounters | The patient's encounters. |
| Clinical History | The patient's clinical history. |
| Appointments | The patient's appointments. |
| Questionnaire Updates | The questionnaire responses recorded for the patient. |

Which of these tabs you see depends on your permissions.

## Expected outcome

- Care shows the patient's profile.
- You read the patient's information in the tabs that your permissions allow.
