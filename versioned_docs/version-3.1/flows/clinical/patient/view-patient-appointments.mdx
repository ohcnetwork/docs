# View a patient's appointments and tokens

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to open Patient Home and view the [appointments](../../../concepts/scheduling/booking.mdx) and queue tokens of a [patient](../../../concepts/clinical/patient) in Care.

The patient's profile also shows the patient's appointments at every facility.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You have a role in an organization of the facility.
- If you use Patient Home, you find the patient with **Search patients**.
- If you use the patient's profile, you can open the patient's record.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can update bookings on Object | Lets you see the **Appointments** tab on Patient Home. Without it, Patient Home has no **Appointments** tab, even if you can list appointments. It also lets you book an appointment with **Schedule Appointment**. **Search patients** in the facility sidebar shows with this permission, or with a permission to list or create encounters. Hold it in a department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization. By default, the Admin (facility role), Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse roles hold it. |
| Can list bookings on Object | On Patient Home, it lets you see the **Upcoming Appointment** card and list the patient's appointments at the facility. Hold it in any organization of the facility. On the patient's profile, it lets you see the **Appointments** tab. Hold it through a role on the patient. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Lets you open the patient's profile, and load the appointments in its **Appointments** tab. Hold it through a role on the patient. By default, the Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse roles hold it. |
| List tokens at the facility | Lets you view the patient's queue tokens. |
| Create encounters | Lets you create an encounter for the patient. |

You hold a role on a patient through an organization of one of the patient's active encounters or of its current location, through a governance organization of the patient, or through a direct link to the patient.

If you cannot open the patient's record through a role on the patient, "Can list patients" in a governance organization of the patient also opens it.

Note: Your deployment's configuration can remove the need for these permissions to open a patient's record. By default, it does not.

To open one appointment from these lists, you need access to it at its facility, as in [Find and open appointments](../../scheduling/appointment/find-appointments.mdx).

## Steps

### 1. Open Patient Home

1. Find the patient with **Search patients**. See [Search for a patient](./search-patient.mdx).
2. Select the patient in the results.
3. If Care shows **Verify Patient Identity**, enter the patient's year of birth in four digits. Then select **Verify**.

![Search patients results for the phone number 90000 00002, with Arjun Rao's row outlined](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-1.png)

![The Verify Patient Identity dialog with the year of birth 1974 entered, above Cancel and Verify](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-2.png)

Care shows Patient Home for that patient.

Patient Home opens on the **Encounters** tab.

### 2. Use the quick actions

Patient Home offers quick actions for the patient. Your permissions control which quick actions appear.

- Select **Schedule Appointment** to book an appointment for the patient. See [Book an appointment for a patient](../../scheduling/appointment/book-appointment.mdx).
- Create an encounter for the patient.
- Generate a queue token for the patient.

![Patient Home for Arjun Rao with the Schedule Appointment quick action outlined, next to Create Encounter and View Accounts](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-3.png)

Note: Whether the "Generate Token" quick action appears on Patient Home can also
depend on your deployment's configuration.

### 3. View the patient's appointments

If the patient has an appointment for today at this facility, the **Upcoming Appointment** card shows one of them. The appointment has a status such as Booked, Checked-In or In-Consultation.

![The Upcoming Appointment card outlined, showing a Booked appointment with Dr. Meera Iyer today and the link View all appointments (2)](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-4.png)

Select the **Appointments** tab.

If the patient has more than one such appointment today, the card shows **View all appointments** and their number. The number counts only today's appointments. The link opens the **Appointments** tab, if you can see that tab.

The **Appointments** tab lists the patient's appointments at this facility only. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Appointments from today on with a status such as Booked, Checked-In or In-Consultation. |
| **Past** | Appointments before today with one of the Upcoming statuses, or with the status Fulfilled or No-show. |
| **Cancelled** | Appointments on any date with the status Cancelled, Entered in Error, Rescheduled or No-show. |

![The Appointments tab of Patient Home on its Upcoming tab, with the Date, Time, Resource and Status of each appointment outlined](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-5.png)

![The Cancelled tab outlined, listing an appointment that is Entered in Error and one that is No-show](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-6.png)

The table shows the **Date**, **Time**, **Resource** and **Status** of each appointment.

On a phone, each appointment is a card with **See Details**.

Select an appointment to open it on **Appointment Details**.

Note: The **Bookings** tab of the **Book Appointment** sheet also lists the patient's appointments.

### 4. View the patient's tokens

Token view on Patient's Home displays all the existing tokens for the patients 

### 5. View the patient's appointments at every facility

1. Open the patient's profile. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).
2. Select the **Appointments** tab.

The tab lists the patient's appointments at every facility. Each appointment also shows its facility.

![The Appointments tab of the patient's profile outlined, with each appointment showing its date, time, practitioner, facility and status](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-7.png)

The tab has the same **Upcoming**, **Past** and **Cancelled** tabs as Patient Home.

Select an appointment to open it at its facility.

![Appointment Details of an appointment opened from the patient's profile, with the page title outlined](/media/clinical/patient/view-patient-appointments-8.png)

## Expected Outcome

- On Patient Home, you see the patient's appointments at this facility under **Upcoming**, **Past** and **Cancelled**.
- On the patient's profile, you see the patient's appointments at every facility.
- You see the patient's queue tokens, for all facilities or for one facility.
- You can open a listed appointment on **Appointment Details**.
