# Open Patient Home

## Overview

This flow describes how to open Patient Home, the page for one [patient](../../../concepts/clinical/patient) inside a facility. Search and registration both end on Patient Home. From it, you start the next task for the patient, such as an encounter or an appointment.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient is registered in Care.
- You have a role in the facility's Administration department or in one of its departments or teams.
- You start from **Search patients**, the registration form, the patient's profile, an encounter or an appointment. Care takes the patient's phone number and the start of the patient's ID from that page.
- If Care asks for the patient's year of birth, you know it. Patient Home shows the patient only if the year is correct.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Tag Config | Open Patient Home, and the profile in step 6. You need it in the facility's Administration department or in one of its departments or teams. A role in a government organization does not count. Patient Home needs no patient permission. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold it. |
| Can list encounters | See the encounter list on the **Encounters** tab. You need it at this facility. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold it. |
| Can list patients | Load the encounter list. You need it through a role on the patient, for example a role in a department of one of the patient's open encounters. A role in the facility's Administration department also counts while the patient has an open encounter at the facility. Step 6: in place of Can view clinical data about patients, it opens the profile when you hold it in a government organization that covers the patient. A role in a facility does not count for this. If you are assigned to the patient on the **Users** tab, you need neither. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Administrator, Facility Admin, Volunteer and Pharmacist roles hold it. Care does not offer the Pharmacist and Facility Admin roles for a government organization. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Step 6: open the patient's profile. You need it through a role on the patient. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold it. |
| Can Create encounter | See **Create Encounter**. You need it at this facility. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold it. |
| Can update bookings on Object | See **Schedule Appointment** and the **Appointments** tab. You need it at this facility. By default, the Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse roles hold it. |
| Can list bookings on Object | See the **Upcoming Appointment** card and load the patient's appointments at this facility. You need it at this facility. By default, the Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can list token on Object | See the patient's tokens at this facility. You need it at this facility. By default, the Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |
| Can Create on Token | See **Generate Token**, if your deployment turns it on. You need it at this facility. By default, the Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator roles hold it. |

Note: "At this facility" means a role in the facility's Administration department or in one of its departments or teams.

## Steps

### 1. Open Patient Home

Open Patient Home in one of these ways.

- From **Search patients**: select the patient. If Care asks for the year of birth, enter it. Then select **Verify**. See [Search for a patient](./search-patient.mdx).
- After you register a patient: Care opens Patient Home. See [Register a patient](./register-patient.mdx).
- From the patient's profile: in the **Actions** panel, select **Patient Home**.
- From an encounter or an appointment: select the patient's name. In the pop-up, select **Patient Home**.

If no patient matches, Care shows **Verification Failed** and "Please enter the correct birth year to verify the patient details."

Care also shows **Verification Failed** if you do not have Can Read Tag Config in this facility. Then Care also shows the message "Cannot view facility tags".

Select **Go Back** to return to the previous page. If there is no previous page in Care, Care opens **Search patients**.

![Patient Home with the patient card, the action buttons, the Encounters tab and the Tokens panel](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-1.png)

### 2. Check the patient card

The patient card shows the patient's name and tags.

If the patient has no tags, the card shows "No tags assigned".

To apply or remove tags, select **Add Tags** or **Manage Tags** on the card. See [Manage patient tags](./manage-patient-tags.mdx).

Care shows **Add Tags** or **Manage Tags** to everyone who opens Patient Home. To save a change, you need the permissions in [Manage patient tags](./manage-patient-tags.mdx).

![The patient card outlined, showing the patient's name, age, sex, a tag and Manage Tags](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-2.png)

### 3. Start a task with the action buttons

Patient Home shows these buttons when your permissions at this facility allow them.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Encounter** | Opens the form to create an encounter for the patient. Keyboard shortcut: `e`. See [Create an encounter](../encounter/create-encounter.mdx). |
| **Schedule Appointment** | Opens the **Book Appointment** panel for the patient. Keyboard shortcut: `s`. |
| **Generate Token** | Shows only when your deployment turns it on. Keyboard shortcut: `t`. See [Create a token](../../scheduling/token/create-token.mdx). |
| **View Accounts** | Opens the facility's active accounts for this patient. Care always shows this button. Keyboard shortcut: `a`. |

Note: A keyboard shortcut works only when its button shows on the page.

![The action buttons Create Encounter, Schedule Appointment and View Accounts, outlined](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-3.png)

### 4. View the patient's encounters

Patient Home opens on the **Encounters** tab.

Select **Active** or **Completed**.

- **Active**: encounters that are Planned, In Progress or On Hold.
- **Completed**: encounters that are Discharged, Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued or Entered in error.

The list shows the patient's encounters at every facility, not only at this facility.

If there are no encounters, Care shows "No active encounters found" or "No completed encounters found".

If you do not have Can list encounters at this facility, Care does not show the list.

To load the list, you also need Can list patients through a role on the patient. See the Permissions section.

![The encounter list outlined, with Active selected and one in-progress encounter with View Encounter](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-4.png)

### 5. View the patient's appointments and tokens

The **Upcoming Appointment** card shows the patient's appointment today at this facility.

If the patient has more than one appointment today, the card has a link to all of them. The link opens the **Appointments** tab.

If you do not have Can update bookings on Object at this facility, Patient Home has no **Appointments** tab.

The **Appointments** tab lists the patient's appointments at this facility.

On a large screen, the patient's tokens at this facility show in a side panel. On a smaller screen, they show in the **Tokens** tab.

The token list shows the tokens of one date, up to 50.

For details, see [View a patient's appointments and tokens](./view-patient-appointments.mdx).

![The Appointments tab of Patient Home, with Upcoming selected and the list of two booked appointments outlined](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-5.png)

### 6. Open the patient's profile

Select the patient's name on the patient card.

In the pop-up, select **View Profile**.

Care opens the patient's profile, **Patient Details**. See [View a patient's profile](./view-patient-profile.mdx).

If Care cannot open the patient for you, the profile shows **Patient Not Found**. Patient Home needs no patient permission, but the profile does.

![The pop-up that opens from the patient's name, with View Profile outlined above the patient's identifier, phone numbers, address and tags](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-6.png)

![The patient's profile, with the Patient Details heading outlined](/media/clinical/patient/open-patient-home-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows Patient Home for the patient. Patient Home has the patient card, the **Encounters** tab and the action buttons that your permissions allow.
- From Patient Home, you can create an encounter, schedule an appointment or open the patient's profile.
