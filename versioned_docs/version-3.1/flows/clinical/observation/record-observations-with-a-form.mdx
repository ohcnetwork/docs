# Record observations with a form

## Overview

This flow describes how to record [observations](../../../concepts/clinical/observation.mdx) for an encounter by filling a [questionnaire](../../../concepts/definitions/questionnaire.mdx). When you save, Care records observations of the encounter from your answers to coded questions.

## Pre-requisites

- The patient has an open [encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) in your facility. An encounter is closed when its status is **Completed**, **Cancelled**, **Discontinued** or **Entered in error**.
- You open the encounter from your facility.
- You work on the current encounter, not on a past encounter that you selected from the encounter history.
- An active questionnaire for encounters is available at your facility. It is shared with your department or with a department below it, as described under Permissions.
- Each question that must record an observation has an observation code. The code is added in the questionnaire builder with **Search for observation codes**. The builder is described in [Add and edit questions](../../definitions/questionnaire/add-and-edit-questions.mdx).
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can submit questionnaire about patient encounters | Lets you save the answers, which records the observations. You need it in a department of the encounter. By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can Read Questionnaires | Lets you find and open the questionnaire. For your facility's questionnaires, you need it in a department that the questionnaire is shared with. A department above that one also counts, and so does the facility's root department "Administration". By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Administrator, Staff, Facility Admin, Volunteer and Pharmacist hold it. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Lets you open the encounter, and shows **Forms**. You hold it through a role on the patient. A role in a department of the patient's open encounter counts. By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Gives the same access as "Can view clinical data about patients" when you hold it in a department of the encounter. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |

Note: A department of the encounter is a department that the encounter belongs to, or a department above it. The facility's root department "Administration" and the departments of the encounter's current location also count.

Note: These role lists are the defaults. Your deployment can change them.

## Steps

### 1. Open the encounter

Open the patient's encounter from your facility.

Go to the **Overview** tab.

![The Overview tab selected in the encounter menu, with the quick actions and the Vitals panel](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-1.png)

### 2. Open Forms

In the quick actions on the **Overview** tab, select **Forms**. You can also press `f`.

Care opens a list of questionnaires with the search box **Search Forms**.

You can also start from **Encounter Actions**. Select **Encounter Actions**, or press `Shift+E`. In the **Questionnaire** group, select a questionnaire of your facility. Care opens the questionnaire, as in step 3. Then continue with step 4.

![The Search Forms box over the encounter, with the Vital Signs questionnaire in the list](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-2.png)

### 3. Choose the questionnaire

Find the questionnaire in the list. To search, type part of its title in **Search Forms**.

Select the questionnaire. Care opens it.

![Vital typed in Search Forms, with the Vital Signs questionnaire in the list](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-3.png)

### 4. Answer the questions

Answer the questions. A question with * after its text is required.

If a question has a unit, the unit shows in brackets after the question.

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Number box | A number. If the question takes whole numbers, Care removes the decimal part. |
| Number box with **Unit** | A quantity: a number and a unit. The question's own unit is selected first. |
| Unit buttons under the number box | The unit of a quantity, when the question offers only a few units. |

Note: Care shows a quantity with the unit that is set on the question, even if you select another unit. If the question shows a unit in brackets, enter the number in that unit.

![The Vital Signs questionnaire with its six required questions answered, each with its unit in brackets after the question](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-4.png)

### 5. Save the answers

Select **Save Changes**.

![The Save Changes button at the top of the questionnaire](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-5.png)

![The Overview tab after saving, with the message Questionnaire submitted successfully and the new values in the newest column of the Vitals panel](/media/clinical/observation/record-observations-with-a-form-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows "Questionnaire submitted successfully".
- Care returns you to the **Overview** tab, or to the encounter tab where you started.
- For each coded question that you answered, Care records one observation.
- For a group of questions set up as components, Care records one observation for the whole group. The answers are stored inside it as components, not as observations of their own.
- Each observation belongs to the encounter and links to the questionnaire response.
- Each observation has the time when you saved, not a time that you enter. Care names you as the person who entered it.

The observations then appear in these places:

- The **Observations** tab lists the encounter's observations by date, with **filed by** and your name. A group set up as components, and the answers inside it, are not listed there. See [View the observations of an encounter](./view-encounter-observations.mdx).
- For an answer chosen from a list, the **Observations** tab shows no value. Select the entry. Then select the **Table** tab of the history panel to see the answer. See [Chart observations over time](./chart-observations-over-time.mdx).
- If the question's code is a vital sign of the **Vitals** panel on the **Overview** tab, the observation appears there. Observations from a questionnaire show no normal range and no flag there. See [Check a patient's vital signs](./check-vital-signs.mdx).
- If a chart set of the **Plots** tab charts the question's code, the observation appears in that chart. The chart plots only numbers. See [Chart observations over time](./chart-observations-over-time.mdx).

These answers do not become observations:

- An answer to a question without an observation code.
- Answers to a questionnaire about the patient rather than an encounter. Care saves the questionnaire response, but records no observations.

To correct a mistake, use the questionnaire response. Care has no control to change or remove one observation that a questionnaire recorded.

- Mark the whole questionnaire response as entered in error. See [Mark a response as entered in error](../questionnaire-response/mark-a-response-as-entered-in-error.mdx).
- Do this within 120 minutes after you save the questionnaire. This is the default limit, and your deployment can set another.
- You need "Can submit questionnaire about patient encounters", and the encounter must be open.
- Care then marks every observation of that response as entered in error.

After this, the **Vitals** panel, the history panel, and the **Graph** and **Recent Data** tabs of the charts leave out these observations. The **Observations** tab and the **Full History** tab of a chart still list them, with no marker.

If you see one of these situations, use this table.

| If you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forms** is not in the quick actions. | The encounter is closed, or you did not open the current encounter from your facility. Or you lack a permission listed above. | Open the current encounter from your facility. If **Forms** is still missing, ask the person who manages user roles in your facility for the permissions listed above. |
| "You do not have permission to view this encounter" | You have no permission that opens this encounter. | Ask the person who manages user roles in your facility for "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data". |
| The questionnaire is not in the list. | The list shows at most 15 active questionnaires that are shared with you. | Type part of its title in **Search Forms**. If it is still missing, ask the person who manages questionnaires in your facility to share it with your department. |
| "This field is required" | A required question has no answer. | Answer each question marked with *. Then select **Save Changes** again. |
| "Nothing to submit yet — answer at least one question" | You did not answer any question. | Answer at least one question. Then select **Save Changes** again. |
| "Failed to submit questionnaire" | The submission failed. Care marks the questions that it rejected. | Correct the marked answers. Check that the encounter is still open. Check that you have the permissions listed above. Then select **Save Changes** again. |
