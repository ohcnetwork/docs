# Close an encounter

This flow marks an active encounter as complete — the step that ends the episode of care and stops new clinical activity from being recorded against it.

:::info Prerequisite
Read [Encounter](../../concepts/clinical/encounter) to understand encounter status and which states are terminal.
:::

## Before you start

- You need a role with **encounter update** permission (typically Admin, Doctor, Nurse, or Facility Admin) on the encounter.
- An inpatient encounter must be discharged from its location before Care lets you mark it complete.
- If the encounter came from a scheduled appointment, closing it can also close the linked appointment and token.

## Steps

### 1. Open the encounter

Go to the patient's encounter. Select **Mark as Complete**.

Use the keyboard shortcut `m` then `c` to open this dialog directly from the encounter.

### 2. Confirm completion

Care shows a confirmation dialog. If the encounter still needs a discharge summary, Care sends you to the discharge questionnaire instead of completing the encounter.

If your facility links the encounter to an appointment or a token, select whether to close only the encounter or close the appointment and token together.

### 3. Confirm the action

Select **Mark as Complete**.

On success, Care sets the encounter's status to **Completed** and records the change in the status history.

## Reopening a completed encounter

If you close an encounter by mistake, or care must resume, select **Restart Encounter** from the encounter's actions menu. Care only allows a restart within the time limit your deployment configures, and only for a completed encounter.

## After closing an encounter

| Next step | When |
| --- | --- |
| Start a new encounter | The patient returns for another episode of care |
| Review the discharge summary | You need to confirm the final clinical record |
| Restart the encounter | Care was closed in error and the time limit has not passed |
