# Take a photo and upload it

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to take a photo with the camera of your device and upload it as a [file](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx) to a patient or to an encounter in Care.

After you keep the photo, Care opens the same upload dialog as in [Upload a file](./upload-a-file.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- Your device has a camera, and you can let your browser use it.
- The patient is registered in Care, and you can open the patient's page or the encounter.
- If you upload to an encounter, you opened the encounter from a facility. Outside a facility, for example from an organization, the encounter's **Files** tab is read-only.
- If you upload to an encounter, you look at the encounter that you opened, not another encounter of the patient.
- If you upload to an encounter, its status is not Completed, Cancelled, Entered in error or Discontinued.
- If you upload to an encounter, the encounter workspace layout does not hide the **Files** tab.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update a Patient's data | Upload a photo to the patient. Care shows **Add Files** on the patient's **Files** tab only with this permission. Hold it through [a role on the patient](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx#permissions). By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Administrator, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Upload a photo to an encounter. Hold it in a [department](../../../concepts/facility/department.mdx) of the encounter or of its current location. Nobody can upload files to a closed encounter. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Update Encounter non clinical | Care shows **Add Files** on the encounter's **Files** tab only with this permission. Hold it through a role on the encounter. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Shows the patient's **Files** tab and lists the patient files. Also shows the encounter's **Files** tab. Hold it through [a role on the patient](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx#permissions). By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Shows the encounter's **Files** tab and lists the encounter files. Hold it in a department of the encounter or of its current location. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |

Note: By default, the Administrator role holds "Can Update a Patient's data" but not "Can view clinical data about patients". So a user who has only the Administrator role cannot open the patient's **Files** tab, and cannot upload files there.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Files tab

To upload to the patient, open the patient's page. Select the **Files** tab.

To upload to an encounter, open the encounter. Select the **Files** tab, or press `g` and then `f`.

Care opens the **Files** sub-tab.

![The Files tab of the Patient Details page, with Add Files](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-1.png)

### 2. Open the camera

Select **Add Files**. Select **Open Camera**.

Care opens the camera. The camera fills the height of the screen.

![Open Camera in the Add Files menu](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-2.png)

### 3. Allow access to the camera

While the browser asks for access, Care shows **Requesting Camera Access** and **Please allow camera permissions in your browser**.

Allow the browser to use the camera.

If access to the camera is denied, Care shows **Camera permission denied** and two steps.

Select the settings icon in the address bar of the browser. Clear the blocked camera permission.

### 4. Choose the camera

Care starts with the back camera.

On a large screen with more than one camera, select a camera in the **Select Camera** menu.

On a small screen, select the switch button to change between the back camera and the front camera.

If the device has no back camera, Care shows **Switch camera is not available.**

### 5. Take the photo

Take the photo. Care shows the photo with three buttons. The buttons show an icon and no text.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Check (confirm) | Keeps the photo, closes the camera, and opens the upload dialog. |
| Retake | Removes the photo and goes back to the camera, so that you can take the photo again. |
| Close | Closes the camera and removes the photo. |

![The camera view with the shutter button and the close button](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-3.png)

:::caution

The retake button and the close button remove all the files that you chose, not only the photo.

:::

### 6. Keep the photo

Select the check button.

Care closes the camera and opens the upload dialog with the photo.

![The photo taken, with the retake, check and close buttons](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-4.png)

### 7. Name and upload the photo

Enter a name in the **File Name** field. The field is required, and it starts empty.

Select **Upload**.

For the other options in the dialog and for the messages that Care can show, see [Upload a file](./upload-a-file.mdx).

![The Upload File dialog with the photo and Wound photo in the File Name field](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-5.png)

![The message File Uploaded Successfully, and Wound photo.png at the top of the Files list](/media/clinical/files/take-a-photo-6.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows **File Uploaded Successfully**.
- The photo shows in the **Files** list of the patient or of the encounter, with the name that you entered.
- The photo is at the top of the list, unless a search or a filter hides it.
