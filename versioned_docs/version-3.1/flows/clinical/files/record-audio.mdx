# Record audio and upload it

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/files/record-audio.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/files/record-audio.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/files/record-audio.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to record audio with the microphone of your device and upload the recording as a [file](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx) to a patient or to an encounter in Care.

After you keep the recording, Care opens the same upload dialog as in [Upload a file](./upload-a-file.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- Your device has a microphone, and you can let your browser use it.
- The patient is registered in Care, and you can open the patient's page or the encounter.
- If you upload to an encounter, you opened the encounter from a facility. Outside a facility, for example from an organization, the encounter's **Files** tab is read-only.
- If you upload to an encounter, you look at the encounter that you opened, not another encounter of the patient.
- If you upload to an encounter, its status is not Completed, Cancelled, Entered in error or Discontinued.
- If you upload to an encounter, the encounter workspace layout does not hide the **Files** tab.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update a Patient's data | Upload a recording to the patient. Care shows **Add Files** on the patient's **Files** tab only with this permission. Hold it through [a role on the patient](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx#permissions). By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Administrator, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Upload a recording to an encounter. Hold it in a [department](../../../concepts/facility/department.mdx) of the encounter or of its current location. Nobody can upload files to a closed encounter. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Update Encounter non clinical | Care shows **Add Files** on the encounter's **Files** tab only with this permission. Hold it through a role on the encounter. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Shows the patient's **Files** tab and lists the patient files. Also shows the encounter's **Files** tab. Hold it through [a role on the patient](../../../concepts/clinical/files.mdx#permissions). By default, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Admin and Facility Admin hold it. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Shows the encounter's **Files** tab and lists the encounter files. Hold it in a department of the encounter or of its current location. By default, Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin hold it. |

Note: By default, the Administrator role holds "Can Update a Patient's data" but not "Can view clinical data about patients". So a user who has only the Administrator role cannot open the patient's **Files** tab, and cannot upload files there.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Files tab

To upload to the patient, open the patient's page. Select the **Files** tab.

To upload to an encounter, open the encounter. Select the **Files** tab, or press `g` and then `f`.

Care opens the **Files** sub-tab.

![The Files tab of the Patient Details page, with Add Files](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-1.png)

### 2. Open the recorder

Select **Add Files**. Select **Record Audio**.

Care opens the recorder. It shows **Record Audio**, **Click the button to start recording**, and a microphone button.

![Record Audio in the Add Files menu](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-2.png)

### 3. Start the recording

Select the microphone button. Care starts to record only when you select this button.

If the browser asks for access to the microphone, allow it.

If access is refused, Care shows **Please grant microphone permission to record audio.**

The recorder then shows **Please allow microphone permission in site settings**. Allow the microphone in the site settings of your browser.

For help, select **Click here to know how to allow**.

![The Record Audio screen with Click the button to start recording and the microphone button](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-3.png)

### 4. Stop the recording

Speak into the microphone. The recorder shows **Recording** and the time that has passed.

To stop, select the button that shows the time. Screen readers name this button **Stop Recording**.

![The Recording screen with the time on the button that stops the recording](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-4.png)

### 5. Listen to the recording

The recorder shows **Audio Recorded** and plays the recording.

Use the player to listen to the recording again.

![The Audio Recorded screen with the audio player](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-5.png)

### 6. Keep the recording or record again

To keep the recording, select **Done**. Care opens the upload dialog with the recording.

If you want a new recording, select **Start Again**.

![The Done and Start Again buttons under the audio player](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-6.png)

### 7. Name and upload the recording

Enter a name in the **File Name** field. The field is required, and it starts empty.

Select **Upload**.

For the other options in the dialog and for the messages that Care can show, see [Upload a file](./upload-a-file.mdx).

![The Upload File dialog with the recording and Voice note in the File Name field](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-7.png)

![The message File Uploaded Successfully, and Voice note.mp3 at the top of the Files list with a Play button](/media/clinical/files/record-audio-8.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care shows **File Uploaded Successfully**.
- The recording shows in the **Files** list of the patient or of the encounter, with the name that you entered.
- The recording is at the top of the list, unless a search or a filter hides it.
- In the list, the **File Type** of the recording is Audio, and its row has a **Play** button. To listen, see [View or preview a file](./view-or-preview-a-file.mdx).
