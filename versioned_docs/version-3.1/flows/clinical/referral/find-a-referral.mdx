# Find a referral

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to find a [referral](../../../concepts/clinical/referral.mdx) in a referral list and open it.

At the receiving end, a facility, a department or an organization has a list of the referrals sent to it. At the sending end, the patient's page and the encounter each have a list of their referrals.

## Pre-requisites

- A referral was sent to your facility, department or organization. Or it was sent for a patient or encounter that you can open.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Referrals | Lets you see a referral list and open a referral. Where you need it depends on the list, as described below. By default, the Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Facility Admin, Nurse, Staff, Manager and Member roles have this permission. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | Needed only for the referrals widget on the encounter's overview. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Admin and Facility Admin roles have this permission. |

Where you need Can Read Referrals:

- For the list of referrals sent to the facility itself, through a role in the facility's root department, Administration.
- For a department's list, through a role in that department or a department above it. A role only in the Administration department does not count for a department.
- For an organization's list, through a role in that organization or an organization above it.
- For the encounter's list, through a role in a department of the encounter or a department above it, or in the Administration department.
- For the patient's list, through a role on the patient.
- To open a referral, at the sending end or at the receiving end.

A role on the patient comes from the organizations of the patient's open encounters and their current locations. It also comes from the patient's own organizations, or from a direct link to the patient.

Being an assignee of a referral does not by itself let you open it. You need Can Read Referrals at one of its ends.

## Steps

### 1. Open a referral list

Open the list that you need.

#### At a facility

In the sidebar, under **Services**, select **Referrals**.

![Referrals under Services in the facility sidebar](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-sidebar.png)

The sidebar shows **Referrals** if you have Can Read Referrals in the facility. A role in any of its departments or teams is enough.

The **Referrals** page shows the referrals addressed directly to the facility.

If your role is only in a department, the page first shows "You do not have access to these referrals". Choose your department in **Filter** to see its referrals.

![The message You do not have access to these referrals on the facility's Referrals page](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-2.png)

To see the referrals of a department, select **Filter**, then **Department**, and choose the department. Care lists the departments under **Available Departments**.

![The Department filter with its list of available departments](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-6.png)

A department's list shows only the referrals sent to that department. It does not include referrals sent to the departments or teams under it.

#### At an organization

On the home page, open the **Governance** or **Responsibilities** tab.

Select the organization.

Open the **Referrals** tab. The tab is shown only if you have Can Read Referrals in that organization.

The **Incoming referrals** page shows the referrals sent to the referral availabilities of the organization.

Without permission, the page shows "You do not have access to these referrals".

#### For a patient

On the patient's page, open the **Referrals** tab.

The tab lists all the referrals of the patient.

#### For an encounter

On the encounter, open the **Referrals** tab.

The tab lists the referrals of this encounter.

On the encounter's overview, the referrals widget shows the 5 newest referrals, unless the widget is set to another number.

The widget is shown only if you have Can Read Referrals on the encounter and Can view clinical data about patients.

To see all of them, select **View all referrals**. Care opens the encounter's **Referrals** tab.

The encounter's workspace can hide the **Referrals** tab. Then **View all referrals** is not shown.

You can have Can Create Referrals but not Can Read Referrals. Then the patient's and the encounter's **Referrals** tabs show only **New Referral**.

### 2. Filter the list

Select **Filter**.

On the facility's **Referrals** page, **Filter** is at the top left of the list. Its menu lists **Department**, **Status** and **Assigned to me**. Other lists have no **Department** filter.

![The Filter menu with Department, Status and Assigned to me](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-4.png)

Choose one or more filters. Care shows the filter that you choose next to **Filter**. To remove the filters, select **Clear all**.

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Referrals with the status that you choose: **Pending**, **In progress**, **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **Rejected**. |
| **Assigned to me** | With **Yes**, only the referrals that you are assigned to. |
| **Department** | The referrals sent to one department. Only on the facility's **Referrals** page. |

Care keeps your filters in the page address.

If no referral matches, Care shows "No referrals match these filters. Try another status or clear the filters." To remove the filters, select **Clear filters**.

### 3. Read the list

The list shows the newest referrals first, 15 on each page.

![A department's referral list with the Referral, Patient, Status, Assigned to and Created Date columns](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-7.png)

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Referral** | The title of the referral, with the name of the referral availability under it. |
| **Patient** | The patient. Only in the lists of a facility, a department or an organization. |
| **Status** | The status of the referral. |
| **Assigned to** | The assignees, or **Unassigned** if there are none. |
| **Created Date** | The date when the referral was created. |

If the list is empty, it shows "No referrals found".

### 4. Open the referral

Select the title of the referral.

Care opens the referral on its own page, without the sidebar.

![A referral page opened from the list](/media/clinical/referral/find-a-referral-8.png)

To go back, select **Back** at the top left.

To copy the address of the referral, select the ... button at the right of the patient's name bar, then select **Copy link**.

If Care shows "Referral details are restricted", you do not have permission to open this referral. Contact your facility administrator for access.

If Care shows "Referral not found", check the link.

## Expected Outcome

The referral page opens. It shows:

- The patient, and the **Originating encounter** when the referral came from an encounter.
- The **Referral letter** header, followed by a short code.
- A progress line from **Pending** through **In progress** to **Completed**. It is not shown for a cancelled or rejected referral.
- **Assigned to**, with the assignees.
- **Reason and clinical context**, when the referral has a note.
- **Request**, with the answers to the request forms, and **Shared records**.
- **Responses**.
- **Referral details**, with **Created By**, **Created Date**, **Last Updated** and **Referral reference**.
- The **Discussion** of the referral, in the side panel.

If you cannot see the patient's chart, the page shows "Additional patient details are not available with your current access."
