# Start a referral and assign the team

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to start work on a [referral](../../../concepts/clinical/referral.mdx) and choose the people assigned to it. When you start a referral, its status changes from Pending to In progress.

When a referral arrives, Care has already assigned the default care team of the referral availability to it. You can keep that team or change it.

## Pre-requisites

- A referral was sent, and you can open its page.
- If you want to start the referral, its status is Pending.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update Referrals | Lets you see **Start referral** and **Manage assignees**, and save the new status and the assignees. By default, the Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Facility Admin, Nurse, Staff, Manager and Member roles have this permission. |
| Can Read Referrals | Lets you open the referral page. By default, the same roles have this permission. |

Note: You need each permission at one end of the referral, the sending end or the receiving end.

- Sending end, referral from an encounter: you need the permission through a role in a department of the encounter or a department above it, or in the facility's root department, Administration. A role in the department of the encounter's current location also counts.
- Sending end, referral sent from the patient's page: you need the permission through a role on the patient.
- Receiving end, referral availability of a facility: you need the permission through a role in the facility's root department, Administration.
- Receiving end, referral availability of a department: you need the permission through a role in that department or a department above it. A role only in the Administration department does not count for a department.
- Receiving end, referral availability of an organization: you need the permission through a role in that organization or an organization above it.

A role on the patient comes from the organizations of the patient's open encounters and their current locations, from the patient's own organizations, or from a direct link to the patient.

## Steps

### 1. Open the referral

Open the referral page. To find it, see [Find a referral](./find-a-referral.mdx).

### 2. Start the referral

If the status is Pending, select **Start referral**. It is the green button with a play icon at the top right, next to the ... button.

![The Start referral button at the right of the patient's name bar](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-2.png)

Care changes the status to In progress. Care does not ask you to confirm.

Care shows the message "Referral updated". The **Start referral** button is no longer shown.

![The message Referral updated and the referral with the status In progress](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-toast.png)

If another person changed the status after you opened the page, Care does not save. Care shows the message "The referral status changed. Review its latest status and try again."

Note: While the referral is Pending, each person who can update it sees **Start referral**. This includes people at the sending end and at the receiving end.

To set another status, see [Change a referral's status](./change-a-referral-status.mdx).

### 3. Open the assignees dialog

In the **Assigned to** section, select **Manage assignees**. It is at the right of the section.

![The Manage assignees button at the right of the Assigned to section](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-4.png)

Care opens the **Manage assignees** dialog. The dialog reads "Choose the clinicians responsible for this referral and their roles."

The dialog lists the current assignees. Each one has an X button that removes the person.

![The Manage assignees dialog with Find team members in, Select Member, Select Role and the current assignees](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-5.png)

### 4. Choose where to find people

Open **Find team members in**.

Select one of your facilities, or select **All accessible users**.

![The Find team members in list with the user's facilities and All accessible users](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-7.png)

If you select a facility, **Select Member** lists the users of that facility.

If you select **All accessible users**, **Select Member** is not limited to one facility.

Note: The dialog starts on the first of your own facilities. Make sure that it shows the facility of the people that you want.

Note: Care shows **Find team members in** only if you belong to a facility.

### 5. Add a person

Select the person in **Select Member**. To find the person, type the name in the search box of the list.

![A name typed in the Select Member search and the matching user](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-8.png)

Select the role of the person in **Select Role**. Care lists practitioner roles. To find a role, type its name.

![Roles found by a search in Select Role](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-role.png)

Select **Add**, to the right of **Select Role**. The button stays disabled until you select a person and a role.

![A member and a role chosen, with the Add button](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-10.png)

Care lists the person and the role below the other assignees.

If the person is already in the list, Care shows the message "Member already added".

Each person must be an active user in Care.

To add more people, do this step again for each person.

Note: Being an assignee of a referral does not by itself let a person open it. The person needs Can Read Referrals at one of its ends.

### 6. Save the assignees

Select **Save Changes** at the bottom right of the dialog. The button stays disabled until you change the list.

![The new assignee in the list and the Save Changes button](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-11.png)

Care closes the dialog and shows the message "Referral updated". **Assigned to** shows each person with the role.

![The Assigned to section listing the assignees with their roles](/media/clinical/referral/start-and-assign-a-referral-assigned.png)

## Expected Outcome

- If you started the referral, its status is In progress.
- The **Assigned to** section of the referral page shows the people that you assigned.
- Referral lists show the assignees in the **Assigned to** column. A referral with no assignees shows "Unassigned".
- In a referral list, the **Assigned to me** filter shows the referrals that are assigned to you.
