# Edit a referral's title or note

## Overview

<video src="/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral.mp4" poster="/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral.poster.png" controls width="720">
  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to change the title or the note of a [referral](../../../concepts/clinical/referral.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the referral. [Find a referral](./find-a-referral.mdx) describes how.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update Referrals | Lets you change the title and the note of the referral. By default, the Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Facility Admin, Nurse, Staff, Manager and Member roles have this permission. |
| Can Read Referrals | Lets you open the referral. By default, the same roles have this permission. |

Note: You need each permission at one end of the referral, the sending end or the receiving end.

- At the sending end, if the referral came from an encounter: you need the permission through a role in a department of the encounter or a department above it, or in the facility's root department, Administration. A role in the department of the encounter's current location also counts.
- At the sending end, if the referral was sent from the patient's page: you need the permission through a role on the patient.
- At the receiving end, if a facility published the referral availability: you need the permission through a role in the facility's root department, Administration.
- At the receiving end, if a department published it: you need the permission through a role in that department or a department above it. A role only in the Administration department does not count for a department.
- At the receiving end, if an organization published it: you need the permission through a role in that organization or an organization above it.

A role on the patient comes from the organizations of the patient's open encounters and their current locations, from the patient's own organizations, or from a direct link to the patient.

## Steps

### 1. Open the referral

Open the referral page. [Find a referral](./find-a-referral.mdx) describes how.

### 2. Open the Edit referral dialog

Select the ... button at the right end of the bar with the patient's name. While the referral is Pending, it is to the right of the green **Start referral** button. Screen readers announce it as **Referral actions**.

![The referral's actions menu open from the ... button, with Edit referral at the top](/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral-actions-button.png)

Select **Edit referral**. It is the first item of the menu.

Care opens the **Edit referral** dialog with the text "Update the referral title and clinical context."

![The Edit referral dialog with Referral title, Note and the Save Changes button](/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral-3.png)

If you cannot update the referral, the menu does not show **Edit referral**.

### 3. Change the title or the note

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| **Referral title** | The title of the referral. Required. Up to 255 characters. |
| **Note** | Free text about the referral. The referral page shows it under **Reason and clinical context**. |

### 4. Save the changes

Select **Save Changes** at the bottom right of the dialog.

**Save Changes** stays disabled until you change a field. It also stays disabled while **Referral title** is empty.

Care saves only the fields that you changed.

Care closes the dialog and shows the message "Referral updated".

![The message Referral updated and the changed note under Reason and clinical context](/media/clinical/referral/edit-a-referral-result.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The referral has the new title or note.
- Referral lists show the new title.
- The referral page shows the note under **Reason and clinical context**. If the note is empty, the page does not show this section.
