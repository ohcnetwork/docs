# Discuss a referral

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />
</video>

This flow describes how to send messages in the discussion of a [referral](../../../concepts/clinical/referral.mdx). People who can open the referral at either end read the same discussion.

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the referral. [Find a referral](./find-a-referral.mdx) describes how.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update Referrals | Lets you start the discussion, send messages and edit your own messages. By default, the Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Facility Admin, Nurse, Staff, Manager and Member roles have this permission. |
| Can Read Referrals | Lets you open the referral and read the messages. By default, the same roles have this permission. |

Note: You need each permission at one end of the referral, the sending end or the receiving end.

- At the sending end, if the referral came from an encounter: you need the permission through a role in a department of the encounter or a department above it, or in the facility's root department, Administration. A role in the department of the encounter's current location also counts.
- At the sending end, if the referral was sent from the patient's page: you need the permission through a role on the patient.
- At the receiving end, if a facility published the referral availability: you need the permission through a role in the facility's root department, Administration.
- At the receiving end, if a department published it: you need the permission through a role in that department or a department above it. A role only in the Administration department does not count for a department.
- At the receiving end, if an organization published it: you need the permission through a role in that organization or an organization above it.

A role on the patient comes from the organizations of the patient's open encounters and their current locations, from the patient's own organizations, or from a direct link to the patient.

## Steps

### 1. Open the referral

Open the referral page. [Find a referral](./find-a-referral.mdx) describes how.

### 2. Open the discussion

The **Discussion** panel is on the right of the referral page, beside the referral details. Its header shows the number of messages.

![The Discussion panel on the right of the referral page, before the first message](/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral-2.png)

If you can read the referral but cannot update it, you can read the messages. Care shows "You do not have permission to send or edit messages in this discussion."

### 3. Send a message

Type your message in **Type your message...** at the bottom of the panel.

Select the green send button with the paper-plane icon, to the right of the box. The button is available only after you type a message.

![A typed message and the send button to the right of the box](/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral-send-button.png)

Care shows your message in the panel, with your name and the time.

If the referral has no discussion yet, your first message starts it. A referral has only one discussion.

### 4. Edit your own message

You can edit a message that you wrote. You cannot edit messages from other people.

On your message, select **Edit message**, under your name and the time.

![The Edit message button on your own message in the Discussion panel](/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral-edit-button.png)

Change the text in the box. Then select **Save Changes** below the box.

![The message text in an edit box with the Save Changes button](/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral-edit-save.png)

The message then shows **Edited** after the time, and an edit history line under the text.

![An edited message with Edited after the time and its edit history](/media/clinical/referral/discuss-a-referral-7.png)

Care keeps the earlier text of an edited message in its history.

Note: You cannot delete a message.

## Expected Outcome

- Your message is in the discussion of the referral.
- People who can open the referral at either end can read the messages.
- The discussion stays with the referral. It does not show among the patient's notes.
