# Respond to a referral

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/clinical/referral/respond-to-a-referral.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />

This flow describes how to answer a [referral](../../../concepts/clinical/referral.mdx) with a response form. You can share records with the response, and you can complete the referral when you save.

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the referral page.
- The referral availability has an active response form.
- If you want to complete the referral when you save, its status is Pending or In progress.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Update Referrals | Lets you see **Respond**, save a response, share response records and complete the referral. By default, the Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Facility Admin, Nurse, Staff, Manager and Member roles have this permission. |
| Can Read Referrals | Lets you open the referral page and its forms. By default, the same roles have this permission. |

Note: You need each permission at one end of the referral, the sending end or the receiving end.

- Sending end, referral from an encounter: you need the permission through a role in a department of the encounter or a department above it, or in the facility's root department, Administration. A role in the department of the encounter's current location also counts.
- Sending end, referral sent from the patient's page: you need the permission through a role on the patient.
- Receiving end, referral availability of a facility: you need the permission through a role in the facility's root department, Administration.
- Receiving end, referral availability of a department: you need the permission through a role in that department or a department above it. A role only in the Administration department does not count for a department.
- Receiving end, referral availability of an organization: you need the permission through a role in that organization or an organization above it.

A role on the patient comes from the organizations of the patient's open encounters and their current locations, from the patient's own organizations, or from a direct link to the patient.

To share an existing record with **Add record**, you also need permission to read that record. See [Share records with a referral](./share-records-with-a-referral.mdx).

## Steps

### 1. Open the respond page

You can open the respond page from three places:

- On the referral page, in **Responses**, select **Respond**. It is the green button at the right of the section. After the first completed response, the button reads **Add response**. Care shows this button in every status of the referral.
- On the referral page, in **Appointment and visit**, select **Respond** on the linked appointment. Care shows it when the appointment has an encounter and the status is Pending or In progress.
- On an encounter that you opened from the referral, in the **Referral review** panel, select **Respond**. Care shows it while the status is Pending or In progress.

Care opens the respond page on the full screen. It has the tabs **Respond** and **Referral and shared records**.

If you cannot update the referral, the respond page shows **Read only** and the message "You can view this referral, but you do not have permission to add a response."

### 2. Choose the form

Care shows the active response forms of the referral availability.

If there is more than one form, select the form in **Form**. If there is only one, Care shows it at once.

If there is no form, Care shows the message "No active forms are configured for this referral."

A person with Can Manage Referral Availabilities can add a response form to the referral availability. See [Change or remove referral availability](./change-referral-availability.mdx).

### 3. Answer the form

Answer the questions of the form. A red asterisk (*) marks a required question.

![The response form on the respond page, with red asterisks on the required questions](/media/clinical/referral/respond-to-a-referral-3.png)

![Answers typed in the response form, and the Draft label on the Respond tab](/media/clinical/referral/respond-to-a-referral-4.png)

The respond page also has a **Referral and shared records** tab.

### 4. Share response records

This step is optional.

Go to the **Response records** section. The section reads "Records are shared immediately and remain attached if you discard unsaved answers."

To share a record, select **Add record** or **Upload file**. For the steps, see [Share records with a referral](./share-records-with-a-referral.mdx).

For **Diagnosis**, **Response** and **Diagnostic report** records, Care shows **Records from** when the response has a **Receiving encounter**. Use it to choose **Receiving encounter** or **All patient records**.

### 5. Save the response

Select one of the two save buttons at the bottom right of the page:

- **Save response** saves your answers. Care shows the message "Form response saved".
- **Save response and complete** saves your answers. Then Care sets the status of the referral to Completed. Care shows the message "Response saved and referral completed".

Care shows **Save response and complete** only while the status is Pending or In progress.

![The Save response and Save response and complete buttons at the bottom right of the respond page](/media/clinical/referral/respond-to-a-referral-5.png)

After **Save response and complete**, Care opens the referral page again. It shows the status Completed.

![The message Response saved and referral completed and the referral with the status Completed](/media/clinical/referral/respond-to-a-referral-toast.png)

Care sets the status to Completed only if the referral is still Pending or In progress when you save.

Note: If you leave the page with unsaved answers, Care asks "Discard response?" and shows "Your unsaved answers will be lost." To stay on the page, select **Keep editing**. Records that you shared in **Response records** stay on the referral.

## Expected Outcome

- Care saves your answers as a response on the referral.
- On the referral page, the **Responses** section offers **Add response** for the next response.
- The **Responses** section shows the records that you shared with the response. You cannot change them there.
- If you selected **Save response and complete**, the status of the referral is Completed. The **Responses** section reads "This referral is completed."
