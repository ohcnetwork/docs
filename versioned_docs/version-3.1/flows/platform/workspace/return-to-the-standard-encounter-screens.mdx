# Go back to the standard encounter screens

## Overview

This flow describes how to stop using your [workspace](../../../concepts/platform/workspace.mdx) and go back to the standard encounter screens. These are the tabs that an [encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) shows when you have no workspace chosen.

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the encounter of the patient. See [Encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) for who can open an encounter.
- You chose a workspace, and the workspace picker shows its name. See [Choose the workspace you use on encounters](./choose-your-encounter-workspace.mdx).
- You need no other permission. See the Permissions section below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| None. Care lets you clear your own choice on a workspace that you can see. | Lets you go back to the standard encounter screens. You also need to be able to open the encounter. See [Encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) for who can open an encounter. |

## Steps

### 1. Open the workspace picker

Open the encounter of the patient.

At the top right of the encounter page, select the workspace picker. The picker shows the name of your workspace.

Care opens the **Workspace** panel.

![The encounter of Ravi Kumar on the Ward rounds tab of the General Medicine rounds workspace, with the workspace picker at the top right outlined](/media/platform/workspace/return-to-the-standard-encounter-screens-1.png)

### 2. Reset to the standard workspace

At the bottom of the panel, select **Reset to standard workspace**.

Care shows **Reset to standard workspace** only while you have a workspace chosen.

![The Workspace panel, with Reset to standard workspace outlined at the bottom](/media/platform/workspace/return-to-the-standard-encounter-screens-2.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The workspace picker shows **Standard encounter**.
- Care opens the **Overview** tab of the encounter.
- Your encounters show the standard tabs.
- Clinical history shows its standard tabs.
- To use a workspace again, choose it in the workspace picker.

![The encounter back on the standard screens: the picker reads Standard encounter, outlined, and the Overview tab shows the symptom, diagnosis and vitals](/media/platform/workspace/return-to-the-standard-encounter-screens-3.png)
