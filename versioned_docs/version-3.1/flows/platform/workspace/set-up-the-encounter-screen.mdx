# Set up the encounter screen with a workspace

## Overview

This flow describes how to set up what a [workspace](../../../concepts/platform/workspace.mdx) shows on an [encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx): its tabs, their layouts and sections, and for each section the title that users see, its options and when it shows. You start from a ready-made starter, arrange the tabs and sections, set up each section, check the result in a preview, and then save it.

## Pre-requisites

- The workspace exists in the facility. To make one, see [Create a workspace](./create-a-workspace.mdx).
- You have a role in one of the facility's departments or teams. A role in Administration, the organization Care creates with every facility, also counts.
- If the workspace is a **Personal workspace**, you created it. Nobody else can open or change a personal workspace.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create/Update Workspaces | For a **Facility workspace**: save the design. You need this permission through a role in the facility's Administration. It also lets you open every facility workspace of the facility, shared or not. By default, only the Facility Admin role has it in a facility. |
| Can Create/Update Workspaces | For a **Department workspace**: save the design. You need this permission through a role in the owner department or in a department above it. A role only in Administration is not enough, unless Administration is the owner department. By default, only the Facility Admin role has it in a facility. |
| Can Create/Update Workspaces | For a **Personal workspace**: save the design. You need this permission through a role in any department or team of the facility, or in its Administration. You must also be the person who created the workspace. By default, only the Facility Admin role has it in a facility. |
| Can Read Workspaces | For a **Department workspace**: open the workspace. You need this permission through a role in the owner department or in a department above it. A role only in Administration is not enough, unless Administration is the owner department. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Staff and Volunteer roles have it in a facility. |
| Can View Organizations | For a Questionnaire Responses section: choose an **Author role**. The list shows a role organization only if you hold this permission through a role in it. A role in an organization above it also counts. A role in a department of the facility does not count. Without such a role, the list is empty. By default, the Admin, Manager and Member roles of role organizations have it. |

With the default roles, this means:

- A Facility Admin in Administration can set up a Facility workspace.
- A Facility Admin in the owner department, or in a department above it, can set up a Department workspace.
- A Doctor in the owner department can open a Department workspace, but Care refuses to save changes to it.
- A Facility Admin whose only role is in Administration cannot open a Department workspace that another department owns.
- Only the person who created a Personal workspace can set it up. That person also needs Can Create/Update Workspaces somewhere in the facility.
- The **Author role** list depends on roles in organizations, not on roles in departments. See [Organisation](../../../concepts/access-governance/organization.mdx).

To give someone a role in a department, see [Add users to a department](../../../flows/facility/department/add-users-to-department.mdx).

## Steps

### 1. Open the workspace

Open the facility from the home page, or from the facility switcher in the header.

In the facility sidebar, select **Settings**.

In the settings sidebar, under **Advanced**, select **Workspaces**.

The list shows **Active** workspaces. To find a **Draft** or **Archived** workspace, select its tab. On a phone, select the status in the list that replaces the tabs.

To find a workspace by its name, type in the **Search** box.

Select the name of the workspace, or select **Edit** in its row.

Care opens the **Edit workspace** page. The **Design** area is below the **General settings** panel.

Note: After you create a workspace, Care opens this page for you.

Note: The list shows only the workspaces that you can see. If the workspace is not in the list, check the permissions above.

![The Draft tab of the Workspaces list of City General Hospital, with the workspace Morning rounds outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-1.png)

### 2. Choose a starter

If the workspace has no design yet, the **Design** area shows **What would you like to design?**

Select one of the starters in the table below.

| Starter | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard encounter** | Marked **Recommended**. An **Overview** tab with sections that you can change, and every other encounter tab as a built-in tab. The main column of **Overview** has Quick Actions, Favorite Forms, App overview content, Draft Forms, Referrals, Allergies, Symptoms, Diagnoses, Procedures, Vitals and Questionnaire Responses. Its side column has Encounter summary. |
| **Ward rounds** | A **Ward rounds** tab. Its main column has Vitals, Diagnoses, Symptoms, Questionnaire Responses and Service Requests. Its side column has Quick Actions, Draft Forms, Care Team, Locations and Encounter Actions. After it come the built-in tabs for medicines, notes and files. |
| **Outpatient visit** | An **Outpatient visit** tab with one full-width column: Quick Actions, Favorite Forms, Vitals, Diagnoses, Questionnaire Responses and Current medication. After it come the built-in tabs for service requests and notes. |
| **Blank** | One empty full-width tab with the name **Overview**. |

Note: A starter changes nothing for users until you save.

After the workspace has a design, two buttons in the **Design** area choose the screen: **Encounter** and **Clinical history**. This flow covers **Encounter**. For the other screen, see [Design a workspace's clinical history screen](./design-a-workspace-clinical-history.mdx).

![The starter picker What would you like to design?, with Blank outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-2.png)

### 3. Arrange the tabs

The **Tabs** list shows the tabs of the screen in the order that the encounter shows them.

A lock icon marks a built-in tab. An eye icon with a line through it marks a hidden tab.

To add a tab, select **Add tab**.

To add a standard Care tab, select it under **Built-in tabs**. A built-in tab that is already in the design is not available.

On the **Encounter** screen, **Built-in tabs** lists the standard encounter tabs and the tabs that installed apps add.

To add your own tab, select **New tab…**. Then fill in the fields below.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tab name** | The name that users see. You must enter a name. Use at most 120 characters. |
| **Icon** | The icon of the tab. Select one of six icons without text: a dashboard, stethoscope, clipboard list, panels, activity line or heart. The dashboard icon is the default. |
| **Address in the URL** | Under **Advanced**. Care makes it from the tab name. Use only lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores, and start with a letter. If you change it, the links to this tab change. |

Select **Add tab**.

A new tab has one empty full-width column.

If another tab uses the same address, Care shows "Another tab already uses this address. Choose a different one under Advanced."

If the address is reserved or not valid, Care shows "This address is reserved or invalid. Choose a different one under Advanced."

A screen can have at most 50 tabs. At the limit, **Add tab** is not available.

To change a tab, select it in the **Tabs** list. Then use the controls after the tab name, as the table below describes.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The switch beside **Shown to users** or **Hidden from users** | Shows or hides the tab. A hidden tab stays in the design. |
| The up arrow (↑) and down arrow (↓) buttons | Change the order of the tabs. |
| The tab settings button, with a sliders icon | Changes the name, icon or address of a tab. Built-in tabs do not have it. Select **Apply changes** to keep your changes. |
| The remove tab button, with a trash can icon | Removes the tab and its sections from the design. Select **Remove** to confirm. You cannot remove the only tab. |

A built-in tab shows "This tab is built into CARE". You can show, hide or move it, but Care manages its contents.

The **Overview** tab is the only built-in tab that offers **Customise this tab**. Select it to add or move sections on that tab. The tab keeps the same name and address.

![The Add tab menu open under Built-in tabs, with Notes outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-3.png)

### 4. Choose the layout of a tab

Select a tab that is not a built-in tab. In the **Layout** row, select a layout from the table below.

| Layout | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| **One column** | One column, **Main**, across the full width. |
| **Main + side** | **Main** at two thirds of the width, and **Side** at one third. |
| **Side + main** | A narrow column at one third of the width, and a wide column at two thirds. |
| **Two columns** | **Left** and **Right**, each at half the width. |

If you choose a layout with fewer columns, Care moves the sections of the extra columns into the last column.

A layout is not available if a column then holds more than 50 sections.

If a tab shows "Custom widths from JSON. Pick a layout to standardise them.", select a layout to use standard widths.

![The Overview tab with the Layout row outlined and Main + side selected, showing the Main and Side columns](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-4.png)

### 5. Add sections

At the bottom of a column, select **Add a section**.

The **Add a section** dialog lists the sections in groups. To find a section, type in **Search sections**.

Select a section. Care adds it to that column.

The table below lists the built-in sections.

| Section | Group | Description in the dialog | Needs an encounter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vitals | Clinical records | Vital signs recorded during this encounter. | Optional |
| Diagnoses | Clinical records | Current diagnoses and chronic conditions. | Optional |
| Symptoms | Clinical records | Symptoms recorded for the encounter. | Optional |
| Procedures | Clinical records | Procedures recorded for the encounter. | Optional |
| Allergies | Clinical records | Allergies, criticality, and verification status. | Optional |
| Questionnaire Responses | Clinical records | Form responses, with optional form and result filters. | Optional |
| Observation chart | Clinical records | One or more charts from the observation charts configuration, without the chart set tabs. | Optional |
| Current medication | Clinical records | The patient's active prescriptions and outside medications with dosage, duration, and prescriber. | No |
| Service Requests | Clinical records | Service requests, filtered by status when needed. | Yes |
| Recent orders | Clinical records | This encounter's recent service requests, with their recorded status and links to order details. | Yes |
| Reports | Clinical records | Available reports and report templates. | Yes |
| App overview content | Clinical records | Overview content contributed by installed CARE apps. | Yes |
| Quick Actions | Actions and forms | Shortcuts to common clinical forms. | Active |
| Favorite Forms | Actions and forms | The clinician’s favorite encounter forms. | Active |
| Draft Forms | Actions and forms | Continue unfinished encounter forms. | Active |
| Encounter Actions | Actions and forms | Actions for managing the current encounter. | Active |
| Referrals | Actions and forms | Recent referrals for the selected encounter, with status and assignment filters. | Yes |
| Encounter Tags | Encounter details | Tags attached to the selected encounter. | Yes |
| Locations | Encounter details | Current location and location history. | Yes |
| Care Team | Encounter details | Clinicians assigned to the encounter. | Yes |
| Departments | Encounter details | Departments and teams linked to the encounter. | Yes |
| Hospitalization | Encounter details | Admission information and encounter dates. | Yes |
| Discharge | Encounter details | Discharge details for the selected encounter. | Yes |
| Audit details | Encounter details | When the encounter was created and updated. | Yes |
| Account | Encounter details | The encounter's billing account and balance. | Yes |
| Encounter summary | Encounter details | The standard details, actions, and discharge-report tabs. | Yes |
| Recent encounters | Patient history | The patient's latest encounters with care team, location, and status, optionally limited to encounters the viewer is caring for. | No |

The last column means:

- No: the section shows the patient's records and needs no encounter.
- Optional: with an encounter, the section shows the records of that encounter. Without one, it shows the patient's records.
- Yes: the section needs an encounter, of any status.
- Active: the section needs an active encounter. It hides when the encounter is closed. It also hides for people who cannot make changes to the encounter.
- Quick Actions is marked "Editors only: hidden for read-only users, except referral shortcuts".

Sections from installed apps show under **From apps**, by their type name.

If no section matches your search, Care shows "No widgets match your search."

A column holds at most 50 sections. A workspace holds at most 200 sections across the **Encounter** and **Clinical history** screens.

![The Add a section dialog with Vitals typed in Search sections and the Vitals section outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-5.png)

### 6. Arrange the sections

To move a section, drag it to another place or column.

You can also select the three-dot button (⋯) of the section. Then select **Move up**, **Move down** or **Move to**. **Move to** shows only when the tab has more than one column.

To copy a section, select **Duplicate** in its menu.

To remove a section, select **Remove section** in its menu.

![The menu of the Questionnaire Responses section open, with Move up outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-6.png)

### 7. Set the title and options of a section

To set up a section, select its title, or the settings button (sliders icon) beside the title.

Care opens the section settings panel. Its heading is the name of the section, with the description of the section below it.

The panel can show one or more of the badges below. They tell you when the section shows.

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Requires an active encounter. Automatically hidden when closed.** | The section shows only while the encounter is active. |
| **Patient-level: works without an active encounter** | The section shows the patient's records and needs no encounter. |
| **Works on history pages** | With an encounter, the section shows the records of that encounter. Without one, it shows the patient's records. |
| **Editors only: hidden for read-only users** | The section hides for people who cannot make changes to the encounter, and outside a facility. |
| **Editors only: hidden for read-only users, except referral shortcuts** | Shown on Quick Actions. The section hides for read-only users, except for its referral shortcuts. |

In **Title shown to users**, enter the title that users see. Use at most 120 characters.

To use the default title of the section, leave the field empty.

If the section has options, they show under **Options**. Set them as the table below describes.

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Vitals | **Vital signs to show**: up to 20 observation codes. Use **Quick add from observation charts** or **Add another observation code**. If you choose none, the section shows the standard vital signs. |
| Questionnaire Responses | **Form slug**, **Form responses only**, **Author role**, **Submitted by** and **Latest results**. **Submitted by** offers **Anyone** or **The person viewing**. |
| Service Requests | **Request status** and **Latest results**. |
| Recent orders | **Category**, **Include completed orders**, **Include orders on hold** and **Latest results**. |
| Current medication | **Medication sources** and **Latest results**. **Medication sources** offers **Prescriptions and outside medications**, **Prescriptions only** or **Outside medications only**. |
| Recent encounters | **Care team filter**, **Care team member username**, **Encounter Class**, **Status**, **Hide the encounter being viewed** and **Latest results**. **Care team filter** offers **All encounters** or **My encounters**. |
| Referrals | **Filter referrals by status**, **Assigned to me** and **Latest results**. The section shows 5 referrals if you set no number. |
| Observation chart | **Chart set**, **Charts to show** (up to 20), **Chart height** (120 to 600 pixels) and **Charts per row** (1 or 2). |
| Encounter summary | **Tabs to show**: which tabs of the summary to show. **Open on**: the tab that opens first. It must be one of the tabs that you show. |
| All other built-in sections | No options. The panel shows "This widget has no configuration options. Existing custom settings are preserved." |

In **Latest results**, enter a number from 1 to 100. For Recent encounters, the number is from 1 to 20. If you leave it empty, more results keep loading, except for Referrals.

If **Open on** is not one of the tabs that you show, Care shows "Choose one of the selected tabs as the default tab."

Note: If Encounter summary shows only its actions tab, it needs an encounter that the viewer can change.

Note: The chart sets for Vitals and Observation chart come from the observation charts configuration of your deployment.

Note: For **Author role**, you choose from role organizations. See the Permissions section above.

![The section settings panel of Questionnaire Responses, with Round notes in Title shown to users, and the Options outlined: Submitted by set to The person viewing and Latest results set to 5](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-7.png)

### 8. Choose when the section shows

This step is for the **Encounter** screen only.

Under **When to show**, select an option in **Show this section**.

| Option | The section shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Whenever it has something to show** | Whenever the section has something to show. No status filter applies. |
| **Only while the encounter is active** | When the encounter has any status that counts as active. |
| **Only after the encounter is closed** | When the encounter has any status that counts as closed. This option is not available for a section that needs an active encounter. |
| **Only for specific encounter statuses…** | When the encounter has one of the statuses that you select. |

If you select **Only for specific encounter statuses…**, Care shows a checkbox for each encounter status. Select the statuses. You cannot clear the last selected status.

Care also hides a section in the cases below. In the preview, a hidden section shows "Hidden in this view" and the reason.

| Reason | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **Requires an encounter** | The section needs an encounter, and there is none. |
| **Requires an active encounter. Automatically hidden when closed.** | The section needs an active encounter, and the encounter is closed. |
| **Hidden by the configured encounter-status filter** | The status of the encounter is not one that you selected. |
| **Hidden when this encounter is read-only or outside a facility** | The section is for people who can make changes to the encounter. The viewer cannot make changes, or the page is outside a facility. |
| **Hidden because no installed app adds content here** | The section is App overview content, and no installed app adds content to it. |

A section that the viewer may not read does not hide. It shows "You do not have permission to view clinical data for this encounter" and "Contact your administrator to grant you access."

To check your choice, select **Closed encounter** in the preview. See step 10.

![The Show this section list open under When to show, with Only while the encounter is active outlined](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-8.png)

### 9. Close the section settings

To edit the settings of the section as JSON, open **Advanced configuration**. If the text is not a valid JSON object, Care shows "Enter a valid JSON object before saving the workspace."

Select **Done**.

**Done** is not available while a setting is not valid.

To remove the section from the design, select **Remove section**.

### 10. Preview the design

The canvas is a layout preview with sample content. It loads no patient records.

Use the controls in the toolbar at the top of the **Design** area to check the design. The table below lists them.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Active encounter** and **Closed encounter** | Switch the preview between an active and a closed encounter. Use **Closed encounter** to see which sections hide when an encounter is closed. |
| The desktop and mobile buttons, with a monitor icon and a phone icon | Switch the preview between a desktop size and a phone size. |
| **Preview with access level** | In the menu of the more options button (⋯). Shows the design as a person with a given access sees it. |
| The undo and redo buttons, with curved arrow icons | Undo or redo a change. Care keeps the last 30 changes. |

The access levels are:

| Access level | What the person can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinical editing** | Read clinical data and patient history. On an active encounter, also make changes. |
| **Clinical read-only** | Read clinical data and patient history. |
| **Encounter read-only, without patient history** | Read clinical data, but not patient history. |
| **Encounter details only** | Read no clinical data. |

A section that the preview hides shows "Hidden in this view" and the reason.

A column that the preview hides reads "Whole column hidden in this view; the other columns widen". An empty column reads "Not shown to users while empty".

![The design previewed as Closed encounter: the Round notes section reads Hidden in this view: Hidden by the configured encounter-status filter, outlined, and the Side column with Quick Actions is hidden](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-9.png)

### 11. Edit the design as JSON (optional)

If you prefer to edit the design as text, select the more options button (⋯) in the toolbar. Then select **Edit as JSON (advanced)**.

Care shows the design in the **Template (JSON)** box. To format the text, select **Format JSON**.

To go back to the visual designer, select the more options button (⋯). Then select **Back to design**.

If the workspace has no design yet, you can also select **Advanced: start from JSON instead** on the starter picker.

If the text is not a JSON object, Care does not save it. It shows "Enter valid JSON with an object at the top level. Arrays, null, and other values are not supported."

If the design is not a valid layout, Care opens it in the JSON editor and shows "This configuration needs the JSON editor. Its content is preserved. Use a valid encounter page configuration to return to the visual editor."

### 12. Save the design

Select **Save Changes**.

**Save Changes** is available only when you have changes, nothing is saving, and every section setting is valid.

If a section setting is not valid, Care names the section and asks you to correct it before you save.

On a wide screen, the header shows "Unsaved configuration changes" or "No changes to save".

If Care cannot save, it shows "Unable to save the workspace. Your changes are still here. Please try again."

If you do not have Can Create/Update Workspaces in the place that the Permissions table names, Care also shows "You are not authorized to create a workspace".

If you leave the page before you save, Care asks "You have unsaved changes. Leave without saving?"

Note: If the workspace is **Active**, people who chose it see the new design after you save. To work on the design out of view, set its status to **Draft** first. While the workspace is **Draft**, Care does not use it on the encounters of the people who chose it. See [Publish, hold back or archive a workspace](./publish-or-archive-a-workspace.mdx).

![The message Workspace updated after selecting Save Changes](/media/platform/workspace/set-up-the-encounter-screen-10.png)

## Expected Outcome

- Care saves the design and shows "Workspace updated".
- If the workspace is **Active**, people who chose it see its tabs on their encounters, in your order, and each section with the title and options that you set.
- A built-in tab shows the standard Care tab. Every other tab shows your sections.
- A hidden tab stays in the design, but users do not see it.
- A section shows only when your **When to show** choice and the rules of the section allow it. When a section is hidden, columns that become empty collapse, and the other columns widen.
- If every tab is hidden, people who chose the workspace see "This workspace has no visible pages."
- The clinical history of a patient keeps the standard tabs until you design that screen.
