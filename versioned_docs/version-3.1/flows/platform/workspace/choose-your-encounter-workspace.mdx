# Choose the workspace you use on encounters

## Overview

This flow describes how to choose the [workspace](../../../concepts/platform/workspace.mdx) that sets the tabs and sections you see on an [encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx).

## Pre-requisites

- You can open the encounter of the patient. See [Encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) for who can open an encounter.
- The workspace that you want has the status **Active**. Care does not offer **Draft** or **Archived** workspaces.
- If the workspace is a facility workspace, one of these is true:
  - It is shared with your department or a department under it. See [Share a facility workspace with departments](./share-a-workspace-with-departments.mdx).
  - You hold Can Create/Update Workspaces in the facility's Administration.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Read Workspaces | See a workspace in the workspace picker, and choose it. Where you must hold it depends on the workspace, as step 2 shows. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Staff and Volunteer roles have it in a facility. In a role organization, the Admin, Manager and Member roles have it by default. In a government organization, the Administrator, Doctor, Nurse, Staff and Volunteer roles have it by default. |
| Can Create/Update Workspaces | Optional. In the facility's Administration, it lets you see and choose every **Active** facility workspace, shared or not. By default, only the Facility Admin role has it in a facility. |

Note: You also need the permissions to open the encounter, where the workspace picker is. See [Encounter](../../../concepts/clinical/encounter.mdx) for who can open an encounter.

## Steps

### 1. Open the workspace picker

Open the encounter of the patient.

After the encounter loads, Care shows the workspace picker at the top right of the encounter page. **Back** is at the top left.

The workspace picker shows **Standard encounter** when you have no workspace chosen. Otherwise, it shows the name of your workspace.

Select the workspace picker.

Care opens the **Workspace** panel. The panel shows your current choice and "Your choice applies to all your encounters and clinical history."

![The encounter of Demo Patient Ravi Kumar, with the workspace picker at the top right, reading Standard encounter, outlined](/media/platform/workspace/choose-your-encounter-workspace-1.png)

### 2. Find the workspace

The panel lists each **Active** workspace that you can see, with its name and description. The list is not limited to the current facility.

To find a workspace by its name, type the name in **Search workspaces by name**.

If no workspace matches, Care shows "No workspaces found".

The list can show these workspaces:

| Workspace | It is in your list when |
| --- | --- |
| Facility workspace | You hold Can Read Workspaces in a department that the workspace is shared with, or in a department above it. If you hold Can Create/Update Workspaces in the facility's Administration, you see every facility workspace of that facility. |
| Department workspace | You hold Can Read Workspaces in the owner department, or in a department above it. |
| Personal workspace | You created the workspace. Nobody else sees it. |
| Deployment-wide workspace | You hold Can Read Workspaces in an organization that the workspace is shared with, or in an organization above it. |

If the list cannot load, Care shows "Unable to load available workspaces. You can continue using the current encounter pages." Select **Try Again**.

![The Workspace panel, outlined, with General typed in the search box and General Medicine rounds listed](/media/platform/workspace/choose-your-encounter-workspace-2.png)

### 3. Choose the workspace

Select the workspace in the list.

Care saves the workspace as your choice. Care then opens the first visible tab of the workspace.

If you select the workspace that is already your choice, Care closes the panel.

If Care shows "Could not select this workspace. Please try again.", select the workspace again.

![The encounter after choosing: the picker reads General Medicine rounds, outlined, and the Ward rounds tab shows the patient's vitals, diagnosis and symptom, with the Medications, Notes and Files tabs beside it](/media/platform/workspace/choose-your-encounter-workspace-3.png)

## Expected Outcome

- The workspace picker shows the name of the workspace.
- The encounter shows the tabs of the workspace. A built-in tab shows the standard Care tab. The other tabs show the sections of the workspace.
- In **Encounter Actions**, the **Go to** group lists only the visible tabs of the workspace.
- The choice applies to all your encounters, in every facility. When you open an encounter at its **Overview** tab, Care moves to the first visible tab of the workspace.
- Clinical history uses the Clinical history tabs of the workspace. If the workspace has no Clinical history tabs, clinical history shows its standard tabs.
- Care saves the choice on the server with your user account.
- If the workspace later gets the status **Draft** or **Archived**, or you lose access to it, Care stops using it on your encounters.
- If a workspace that you chose earlier becomes **Active** again, Care can show it in the picker instead of your latest choice. If this happens, choose your workspace again.
- You can choose another workspace later, or [go back to the standard encounter screens](./return-to-the-standard-encounter-screens.mdx).
- If the workspace has no visible tabs, the encounter shows "This workspace has no visible pages."
- If parts of the workspace are not valid, the encounter shows "Some parts of this workspace configuration are invalid. Valid pages and widgets are still shown."
- In both of these cases, you can choose another workspace, or go back to the standard encounter screens.
- If Care cannot load your choice, the encounter shows "Unable to load your default workspace. The standard encounter pages are shown." It also shows **Try Again**.
