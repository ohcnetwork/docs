# Update or retire product knowledge

## Overview

  <track kind="captions" src="/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry.vtt" srcLang="en" label="English" default />

This flow describes how to change the details of [Product Knowledge](../../../concepts/definitions/product-knowledge.mdx) in Care. It also describes how to retire Product Knowledge that the facility no longer uses.

## Pre-requisites

- The facility has the Product Knowledge that you want to change.
- You belong to a department of the facility.
- If you retire the Product Knowledge, its status is not Retired.
- You have the permissions listed below.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create Product Knowledge on Facility | Lets you save your changes and retire the Product Knowledge. You must hold it in the facility's root organization (**Administration**). By default, the Facility Admin role holds this permission. |
| Can Read Product Knowledge | Lets you see the Product Knowledge in a category, open its page, and open the edit form. You can hold it in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Resource Category | Lets you open the categories, and select one in the **Category** field. You can hold it in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |

Note: Care shows **Edit** and **Delete** to every user who can open the Product Knowledge. If you do not hold the create permission in the root organization, Care does not save the change.

## Steps

### 1. Find the Product Knowledge

1. Open your facility.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. In the **Inventory** group, select **Product Knowledge**.
4. Select the category that holds the Product Knowledge. If it has subcategories, open them until you reach the Product Knowledge.

Note: A category lists only Active Product Knowledge at first. To find Draft or Retired Product Knowledge, change the **Status** filter.

Note: To find Product Knowledge by name in a category, type in **Search products**.

![Step 1, Find the Product Knowledge: the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-1.png)

### 2. Open the Product Knowledge

Select **View** on the row of the Product Knowledge. Care opens the Product Knowledge page.

The page shows the details in the **Overview**, **Names**, **Storage Guidelines**, and **Product Definition** cards.

Note: You can also open the Product Knowledge from a product. If the Product Knowledge belongs to your facility, select **View Details** on the page of the product.

![Step 2, Open the Product Knowledge: the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-2.png)

### 3. Update the details

1. Select **Edit**. Care opens the **Edit Product Knowledge** form.
2. Change the fields that you want to update.
3. Select **Update**.

The form has the same fields as the create form. For a description of each field, see [Create product knowledge](./add-product-to-catalog.mdx).

Note: **Update** stays unavailable until you change a field.

Note: If you change the **Slug**, the web address of the Product Knowledge page changes.

Note: You can also select **Edit** on the row of the Product Knowledge in the category.

![Step 3, Update the details (1 of 3): the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-3.png)

![Step 3, Update the details (2 of 3): the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-4.png)

![Step 3, Update the details (3 of 3): the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-5.png)

### 4. Retire the Product Knowledge

Do this step only when the facility no longer uses the Product Knowledge.

1. On the Product Knowledge page, select **Delete**. Care shows this button only when the status is not Retired.
2. Care asks you to confirm. Select **Confirm**.

Note: **Delete** does not remove the Product Knowledge. Care sets its status to Retired.

Note: You can also retire Product Knowledge from the edit form. Set **Status** to **Retired**. Then select **Update**.

Note: To use retired Product Knowledge again, open the edit form and set **Status** to **Active**. Then select **Update**.

![Step 4, Retire the Product Knowledge (1 of 2): the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-6.png)

![Step 4, Retire the Product Knowledge (2 of 2): the screen at this step, with the control for the step outlined](/media/supply/product/edit-retire-catalog-entry-7.png)

## Expected Outcome

- After an update, Care shows **Product knowledge updated successfully** and opens the Product Knowledge page with your changes.
- After you retire the Product Knowledge, Care shows **Product knowledge deleted successfully** and returns to the category.
- Retired Product Knowledge stays in Care. The category lists it only when you set the **Status** filter to Retired.
- Pickers no longer offer retired Product Knowledge, for example when you create a product.
