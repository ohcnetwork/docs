# Appointment Booking

## Definition

An **[appointment](https://build.fhir.org/appointment.html)** in Care reserves a place in one slot of a [schedule template](../scheduling/schedule.mdx) for one patient. The slot belongs to a practitioner, a location or a healthcare service.

Staff book appointments from the patient's records, and patients can book the slots of public templates themselves.

The schedule template makes the slots, and its **Patients per Slot** sets how many appointments one slot takes. Care refuses a [schedule exception](../scheduling/exception.mdx) when a slot that has an appointment starts within the exception's times on one of its dates.

In the names of its permissions and in some messages, Care calls an appointment a booking.

## Key Attributes

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Patient | The patient who has the appointment. |
| Resource | The practitioner, location or healthcare service that the patient sees. |
| Slot | The date and the time of the appointment, in a session of a schedule template. |
| Status | Where the appointment stands, for example Booked or Checked-In. See Status below. |
| Note | Free text about the appointment. |
| Tags | Labels that you attach to the appointment. |
| Booked by | The staff member who booked the appointment, or the patient. |
| Token | The queue token of the appointment, if staff generated one. |
| Encounter | The encounter that started from the appointment, if there is one. |
| Charge item | The charge for the appointment, if the schedule template has charges. |

### Patient

Staff book an appointment from the patient. **Schedule Appointment** is on Patient Home, in the header of the patient page, and on each patient in the patient search results.

Your deployment can also open the booking sheet right after a patient is registered.

A patient can hold only a limited number of upcoming appointments that are still open. Appointments that are Fulfilled, No-show, Entered in Error, Cancelled or Rescheduled do not count.

Care counts the patient's appointments at all facilities. The default limit is 10, and your deployment can change it.

### Slot

In the **Book Appointment** sheet you choose the resource type, the resource, a day and a slot. Then you select **Confirm Appointment**.

The calendar does not let you choose days before today, fully booked days or days without slots. The slot list hides slots that have ended or are full.

Care checks these rules when you confirm:

- The slot has not ended.
- The slot still has a free place.
- The patient does not already have an appointment in that slot.
- The patient is below the limit of upcoming appointments.

A new appointment starts as Booked and takes one place in the slot. Care then opens it in **Appointment Details**.

### Note

When you book, the reason for visit becomes the note of the appointment. **Appointment Details** shows the note.

**Mark as no-show** and **Cancel Appointment** each ask for a note. The note starts with the current note and replaces it.

**Reschedule** asks for a note for the current appointment and a note for the new appointment.

### Tags

You can add [tags](../platform/tagging.mdx) when you book an appointment, and change them while you reschedule. On **Appointment Details**, use **Add Tags** or **Manage Tags**.

The **Appointments** page can filter appointments by tags.

### Booked by

For an appointment that staff book, **Booked by** shows the staff member.

Patients can book appointments themselves in the patient portal, but only in the slots of public schedule templates. They sign in with a one-time password (OTP) on their phone number, and they can book only for patient records with that phone number. In the portal a patient can cancel an appointment, and reschedule it unless it is In-Consultation. For such an appointment, **Booked by** shows the patient's name with "(Patient)". Your deployment can switch off patient sign-in.

### Token

Staff can generate one queue [token](../scheduling/token.mdx) from an appointment that is not in a final status, and the token stays linked to the appointment.

### Encounter

An appointment can lead to one [encounter](../clinical/encounter.mdx). The encounter must be for the same patient and at the same facility.

**Start Consultation** in **Quick Actions** opens the encounter form when the appointment has no encounter yet. The form uses the encounter defaults of the schedule template. When you save the encounter, Care links it to the appointment.

If you hold Can update bookings on Object in the right place, the appointment's status then becomes In-Consultation. Without it, Care still creates and links the encounter, but the status of the appointment does not change.

If an encounter is already linked, **Start Consultation** opens that encounter. It also sets In-Consultation, again only if you hold Can update bookings on Object in the right place.

**Create Planned Encounter** also creates a linked encounter. It shows only while no encounter is linked.

Linking an encounter does not change the status of the appointment by itself. Care refuses to link a second encounter to the same appointment.

From the encounter page, **Close Appointment** sets the appointment to Fulfilled.

### Who can work with appointments

| Permission | What it lets you do | Default roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can list bookings on Object | See appointments on the **Appointments** pages and open **Appointment Details**. You must hold it in the place listed below for the resource. | Admin (facility role), Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator |
| Can update bookings on Object | Book an appointment. For booking, a role in any department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization, counts. Also check in, set In-Consultation, mark Fulfilled or No-show, cancel, mark as entered in error, and change tags. For these, you must hold it in the place listed below for the resource. | Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse |
| Can reschedule bookings on Object | Reschedule an appointment. You also need Can update bookings on Object for the current appointment. For this permission, a role in any department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization, counts. | Admin, Staff, Facility Admin, Doctor, Nurse and Administrator |
| Can Create encounter | Create the encounter from **Start Consultation** or **Create Planned Encounter**. A role in any department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization, counts. | Admin, Facility Admin, Doctor and Nurse |

Care accepts Can list bookings on Object and Can update bookings on Object for an appointment only in these places:

- For a practitioner: the facility's Administration organization, a department or team that the practitioner belongs to, or a parent of it.
- For a location: a department linked to the location or to its parent location, a parent of that department, or the facility's Administration organization.
- For a healthcare service with a managing organization: that organization or a parent of it. The Administration organization does not count.
- For a healthcare service without a managing organization: only the facility's Administration organization.

Only roles that you hold in the facility's organizations count. A role in a governance organization above the facility does not count.

An Administrator can see appointments but cannot book, change or reschedule them, because that role does not hold Can update bookings on Object. A user whose only role is Staff can check in a patient, but cannot create the encounter.

The screen shows **Check-In**, the three-dots menu button and **Reschedule** if you hold the permission in any department or team of the facility, or in its Administration organization. **Add Tags**, **Manage Tags**, **Start Consultation** and **Create Planned Encounter** show without a permission check. Care saves the change only when you hold the permission in the right place.

### Status

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Booked | The slot is reserved for the patient. Every new appointment starts in this status. |
| Checked-In | Staff checked the patient in. **Check-In** sets this status. |
| In-Consultation | The consultation has started. **Start Consultation** sets this status if you hold Can update bookings on Object in the right place. |
| Fulfilled | The visit is complete. **Mark as Fulfilled**, or **Close Appointment** on the encounter page, sets this status. |
| No-show | The patient did not come. **Mark as no-show** sets this status. |
| Cancelled | The appointment is cancelled. **Cancel Appointment** sets this status. |
| Entered in Error | The appointment was recorded by mistake. **Mark as entered in error** sets this status. |
| Rescheduled | The appointment moved to another slot. **Reschedule** sets this status and creates a new appointment. |

Care itself does not enforce an order of statuses. It accepts a change from any status to any other status, also from Cancelled, Fulfilled or No-show, but not a change to Cancelled, Entered in Error or Rescheduled. Those three statuses are set only when an appointment is cancelled, marked as entered in error or rescheduled.

The **Appointment Details** screen is stricter:

- **Check-In** shows only for a Booked appointment. It works from one day before to one day after the start of the slot. This is the check-in window.
- **Start Consultation** shows for a Booked or Checked-In appointment within the check-in window.
- **Mark as Fulfilled** and **Mark as no-show** work from one day before the start of the slot. **Mark as no-show** shows only for Booked and Checked-In appointments.
- **Cancel Appointment** and **Reschedule** are hidden while the appointment is In-Consultation.
- For a Fulfilled, No-show, Cancelled, Entered in Error or Rescheduled appointment, the screen hides **Check-In** and the three-dots menu button.

**Mark as Fulfilled**, **Mark as no-show**, **Cancel Appointment**, **Mark as entered in error** and **Reschedule** are in the menu that opens from the three-dots menu button at the top of **Appointment Details**.

On the **Appointments** page, the board columns and the list tabs are Booked, Checked-In, In-Consultation, Fulfilled and Non Fulfilled. Non Fulfilled holds the Cancelled, Entered in Error, Rescheduled and No-show appointments.

**Cancel Appointment** cancels the appointment. You must enter a note. Care refuses to cancel an appointment that is In-Consultation, with the message "You cannot cancel an appointment In-Consultation".

**Mark as entered in error** sets the status Entered in Error. Its dialog has no note field. The menu offers it also for an appointment that is In-Consultation, but Care refuses it in that status too.

**Reschedule** moves an appointment to another slot. You first enter a note for the current appointment, and then choose a new slot. The new slot can belong to another resource of the same type in the same facility, but it cannot be the current slot.

Care marks the current appointment Rescheduled and frees its place. Care creates a new Booked appointment on the new slot, with the same checks as a new booking, and opens it. An appointment that is In-Consultation cannot be rescheduled.

When an appointment is cancelled, entered in error or rescheduled, Care frees its place in the slot and aborts its charge item.
