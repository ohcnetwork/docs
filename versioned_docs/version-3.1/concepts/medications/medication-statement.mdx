# Medication statement

## Definition

A **[medication statement](https://build.fhir.org/medicationstatement.html)** in Care records a medicine that a patient takes, took or intends to take, and who gave that information. Unlike a [medication request](./medication-request.mdx), a medication statement is not an order: it has no requester, dosage schedule or dispense status. Care records every medication statement against an encounter of the patient.

## Key Attributes

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Medication | The medicine that the patient takes, from the clinical drug list. It is required, and you cannot change it after you save. |
| Source | Who gave the information: **Patient**, **Practitioner** or **Related Person**. The form selects **Patient** for a new statement. You cannot change it after you save. |
| Status | The state of the statement. See Status below. It is required. The form selects Active for a new statement, and you can change the status after you save. |
| Dosage Instructions | Free text that describes how the patient takes the medicine. The form requires it. You cannot change it after you save. |
| Medication Taken Between | The start date and the end date of the period in which the patient takes the medicine. The form requires the start date. Care shows **Ongoing** when there is no end date. |
| Reason | Why the patient takes the medicine, as free text. The form accepts up to 100 characters. You cannot change it after you save. |
| Note | Free text about the medicine. You can change it after you save. |

### How you record medication statements

You record medication statements with an encounter form that contains the **Medication Statement** question. Your facility must first set up such a form, and the form builder allows this question only on an encounter form.

You open the form from **Forms** on the encounter's **Overview** tab, while the encounter is active. The form lists the statements already recorded in this encounter, and you add new ones there.

The patient's **Add Patient Updates** page cannot save a medication statement, because a statement belongs to an encounter.

### Changes after you save

After you save a statement, Care accepts changes to its status, its period and its note. The medicine, the source, the dosage instructions and the reason stay fixed.

The form is stricter than that. For a saved statement it lets you change only the **Status** and the **Note**, and shows the dates read-only.

You mark a statement that was recorded by mistake by setting its status to Entered in Error. The form offers this status only for a statement that is already saved.

### Where Care shows medication statements

The **Medication Statements** sub-tab of the encounter's **Medications** tab lists the statements of the patient from every encounter, not only from the current one.

The patient's **Past Medications** tab shows the same list under **Medication Statements**.

When you prescribe, **Medication History** in the "Medication Request" form also lists the patient's statements. You can add a statement's medicine to the new prescription from there.

### Status

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Active | The patient takes the medicine. New statements start with this status. |
| On Hold | The patient's use of the medicine is on hold. |
| Completed | The patient completed the medicine. |
| Stopped | The patient stopped the medicine. |
| Intended | The patient intends to take the medicine. |
| Not Taken | The patient did not take the medicine. |
| Unknown | It is not known whether the patient takes the medicine. |
| Entered in Error | The statement was recorded by mistake. Care keeps it, but leaves it out of the **Medication Statements** lists and **Medication History**. |

## Permissions

Care gives permissions through the roles that a user holds in departments. The roles named below hold each permission by default; an installation can change the permissions of its roles.

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Update Encounter related clinical data | Record a statement, change a saved statement, and mark it as Entered in Error. You must hold it in one of the encounter's departments or a department of its current location. The encounter must not be Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued or Entered in Error. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can view clinical data about patients | See the patient's statements in the **Medication Statements** sub-tab, on the **Past Medications** tab and in **Medication History**. These lists cover the whole patient, so only this permission opens them. It counts through the patient: through a department of one of the patient's open encounters, through the patient's own organizations, or through a direct link to the patient. After an encounter is closed, its departments no longer count. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read encounter related clinical data | Lets the form load the statements already recorded in this encounter, instead of "Can view clinical data about patients". You must hold it in one of the encounter's departments or a department of its current location. By default, the Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |
| Can submit questionnaire about patient encounters | Needed only when the form also has other questions and you answer them, because Care then saves those answers as well. You must hold it in one of the encounter's departments or a department of its current location, and the encounter must be open. By default, the Staff, Doctor, Nurse and Facility Admin roles hold this permission. |

The Pharmacist role holds none of these permissions by default, and there is no pharmacist access to medication statements.
