# Product

## Definition

A **Product** in Care is one batch of a [Product Knowledge](../definitions/product-knowledge.mdx) that your facility holds, with its own lot number, expiration date, pack size, and purchase price.

Product Knowledge describes what an item is, for example a medication. A product records one batch of that item that your facility received from a supplier.

Care creates products when you receive stock in a purchase delivery.

## Key Attributes

| Components | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Product Knowledge | The Product Knowledge that the product is a batch of. Care sets it when it creates the product, and you cannot change it later. |
| Lot Number | The manufacturing lot or batch number. When you receive stock, you enter it in the **Batch #** field. |
| Expiration Date | The date after which the product should not be used. When you receive stock, you enter it in the **Expiry** column. |
| Standard Pack Size | The pack size of the batch. Care takes it from the **Pack Size** column of the delivery row. |
| Purchase Price | The purchase price of the batch. Care takes it from the **Purchase** columns of the delivery row. |
| Charge Item Definition | The [charge item definition](../definitions/charge-item-definition.mdx) that Care uses to price the product when it bills a patient. This field is optional. |
| Status | Active, Inactive, or Entered in Error. See Status below. |

### Product Knowledge

Each product belongs to one Product Knowledge. One Product Knowledge can have many products, such as the different batches that your facility receives.

A product can use a Product Knowledge of your facility, or one that is shared across the whole Care instance.

You manage Product Knowledge in **Settings**, in the **Inventory** group, under **Product Knowledge**. For its attributes and statuses, see [Product Knowledge](../definitions/product-knowledge.mdx).

### Where you see products

You see the products of your facility in **Settings**, in the **Inventory** group, under **Product**. From the **Products** list, you can view or edit each product.

The edit form does not show the Product Knowledge field. Care has no delete action for products.

### How Care creates a product

You do not create a product in **Settings**. Care creates products when you receive stock from a supplier in **Purchase Deliveries**.

**Purchase Deliveries** is in the **Inventory** menu of a store location, under **External Supply**. You can add items to a purchase delivery while its status is **Draft**.

In each row, you select a Product Knowledge in the **Product** column. Care then fills the row from the most recent Active product of that Product Knowledge in your facility.

When you select **Save Changes**, Care creates a new product for a row in these cases:

- Your facility has no Active product of that Product Knowledge.
- You change the batch details in the row: the batch number, expiration date, pack size, prices, taxes, or discounts.

When you type a new batch number or change the expiration date, the row shows **New**.

The new product is Active. It takes its lot number, expiration date, pack size, and purchase price from the row.

Care can also create a new charge item definition for the new product. It copies the charge item definition of the product that filled the row, if that product has one. If no product filled the row, or you change the **Item Price**, taxes, or discounts, Care takes the prices from the row.

A transfer between two locations of your facility does not create a product. It uses stock that already exists.

### Stock and billing

An [inventory item](./inventory-item.mdx) counts how much of one product is in stock at one location. Care creates it the first time the product arrives at that location.

When staff dispense a medication from stock whose product has a charge item definition, Care creates a charge item for the dispensed quantity. The charge item is Billable, and Care adds it to the patient's default account.

If the product has no charge item definition, Care creates no charge item for the dispense.

When a patient returns dispensed items and the return is completed, Care creates a refund charge item from the charge item definition of each returned product. Care skips a product that has no charge item definition.

### Status

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Care offers the product as an existing batch when you receive stock. |
| Inactive | Care keeps the product, but does not offer it when you receive stock. |
| Entered in Error | Care keeps the product, but does not offer it when you receive stock. |

Note: The **Products** list shows only Active products until you change the **Status** filter.

Note: To stop Care from offering a product when you receive stock, edit the product. Set its status to Inactive or Entered in Error.

## Permissions

| Permission | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Can Create Product on Facility | Edit a product, and save a purchase delivery row that creates a new product. Care checks this permission in the facility's root organization, Administration. By default, the Facility Admin role holds this permission. |
| Can Read Product | See the products of your facility in **Settings** and open each product. In a purchase delivery row, see the existing batches of a Product Knowledge. Care checks this permission in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Product Knowledge | Select a Product Knowledge in a purchase delivery row. Care checks this permission in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Resource Category | Browse the categories in the Product Knowledge and charge item definition pickers, and select a **Category** in a purchase delivery row. Care checks this permission in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Charge Item Definition | Select a charge item definition in the product edit form. Care checks this permission in any department of the facility. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Create Charge Item Definition on Facility | Save a purchase delivery row for which Care creates a charge item definition, or save a new charge item definition from **Create New** in the product edit form. Care checks this permission in the facility's root organization, Administration. By default, the Facility Admin role holds this permission. |
| Can List Facility Locations | Open the store location that holds **Purchase Deliveries**. Care checks this permission in the departments of that location. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Supply Delivery | Open a purchase delivery of the store location. Care checks this permission in the departments of that location. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Read Inventory Item | In a purchase delivery row, see the stock of each existing batch at the store location. Care checks this permission in the departments of that location. By default, the Facility Admin, Administrator, Staff, Doctor, Nurse, Volunteer, and Pharmacist roles hold this permission. |
| Can Create External Supply Delivery on Facility | Save the items of a purchase delivery. Care checks this permission in the departments of the store location. By default, the Facility Admin role holds this permission. |

The departments of a location are the departments linked to it or to a location that contains it, the parents of those departments, and the root organization.

Care counts only the roles that you hold in the facility's departments. Care shows **Edit** to every user who can open a product. If you hold Can Create Product on Facility only in a department other than the root organization, Care does not save your changes.
